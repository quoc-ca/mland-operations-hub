## Readiness assessment

This is **documentation work**, so it is valid to record gaps. It is **not
ready for implementation**: R04 (approved scoped artefact set), R06–R15
(traceable model/contract/authorization/state/UI), R17 (cross-artifact
consistency) and R18 (no material unknowns) are not met. The blockers above
must be resolved, then `spec.md`, `research.md` as needed, `data-model.md`,
OpenAPI, `plan.md`, `tasks.md`, and `quickstart.md` must be regenerated and
analysed together before the backend or frontend personas begin code work.

## Reality check

The apparent shortcut—patching `participant_count` into extra tables while
retaining the old endpoints and completed checkboxes—would hide a change to the
commercial aggregate, capacity invariant and customer contract. It would create
a feature that can look complete in a demo while allocating tickets, deposits
or sessions incorrectly in production. The smallest credible next step is to
settle B01–B04, then write one coherent 003.1 replacement design; it is not to
resume the legacy tasks piecemeal.
