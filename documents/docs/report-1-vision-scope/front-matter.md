**Known Mland context:** Mland offers personalised jewellery-making workshops focused on rings. Booking and quote interactions are currently handled manually through channels such as Instagram and WhatsApp.

> **Stakeholder statement — 16 September 2026:** Mland currently operates two workshop locations. A proposed operating day has three 150-minute sessions: 09:30–12:00, 13:00–15:30, and 16:00–18:30. A proposed attendance rule charges 100,000 VND for each unbooked additional person who actually participates in making the product; staff confirm participation at check-in. A work-in-progress product may be held by the shop and continued under a later booking created by staff. These statements require group approval before becoming baseline requirements.

> **TBD — Mland decision required:** approve capacity and product capability by location, cancellation/no-show rules, attendance-fee exceptions, custody and continuation rules, ring-component catalogue, prices, feasibility constraints and thresholds, review coverage, data retention, Google review governance, and V1 success measures.
