## 4.1 Major Features

| ID | Feature Name | Description | Addresses Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FE-01 | Group Session Booking | Guests select a fixed-capacity ring-workshop session and receive an email confirmation plus tracking code. | GAP-01 |
| FE-02 | Ring Catalogue and Configuration | Customers choose a confirmed ring model or available components; staff can continue design consultation in person. | GAP-01 |
| FE-03 | Image-Assisted Feature Intake | With explicit consent, a reference image is sent to an external AI Vision API for quality and candidate-feature extraction. | GAP-01 |
| FE-04 | Feasibility Triage and Override Audit | Custom designs outside the confirmed catalogue are auto-accepted, staff-reviewed, or auto-rejected from configured feasibility rules; overrides record their reason and decision context. | GAP-01 |
| FE-05 | Estimate and Final-Price Consent | MOH displays an estimate after feasibility is known and records the customer's consent before a final invoice differs from that estimate. | GAP-01 |
| FE-06 | Ring Fulfilment and Collection | Customers choose self-making at the workshop or a shop-made order; shop-made orders move through simple fulfilment statuses to pickup. | GAP-01 |
| FE-07 | Manual Collection Payment Record | Staff record cash or bank-transfer payment after collection without payment-gateway integration or card-data handling. | GAP-01 |
