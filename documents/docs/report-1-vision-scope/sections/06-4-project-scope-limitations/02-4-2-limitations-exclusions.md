## 4.2 Current Baseline Limitations & Candidate Domains

| ID | Candidate Domain | Current Baseline Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LI-01 | Staff and HR management | Staff scheduling, attendance, payroll, contracts, and personnel administration are out of scope. |
| LI-02 | Product and production breadth | V1 supports rings only and Member ready-ring retail with one-unit reservation; it excludes retail catalogue breadth, full inventory, and detailed production scheduling. |
| LI-03 | Payment and accounting integration | V1 uses a Payment Gateway for workshop deposits and Member retail full payment, but excludes card-data storage, POS, tax, accounting, bank reconciliation, and unapproved refund rules. |
| LI-04 | Advanced design tooling | V1 excludes CAD/3D editing and does not claim to reconstruct a ring exactly from a reference image. |
| LI-05 | AI accuracy and autonomous certainty | The AI/feasibility engine is a research prototype under validation; no accuracy claim, final threshold, formula, or dataset result is committed without evidence. |
| LI-06 | Future commercial expansion | Jewellery types other than rings, loyalty, marketing, and forecasting are deferred. Members have no loyalty points in V1. |
| LI-07 | Delivery and review governance | Third-party delivery stops at staff-recorded handoff; carrier integration, live shipment tracking, return, and shipping-refund logistics are excluded. Google Reviews import is research-gated and cannot automatically calculate salary or bonuses. |
