# 1. Product Background

Mland is a jewellery-making workshop specialising in guided silver-ring experiences and the sale of other jewellery items. It serves domestic and international visitors, with guests commonly arriving from the United Kingdom, France, Germany, and Vietnam. Mland currently receives customers at two locations, is considering a third location in Da Nang, and handles approximately 20–30 customers per day at each location. Each location has at least two customer-service staff members.

Today, essential operating work is fragmented across messages, calls, and manual records. A customer who wants to reserve a workshop session must contact the business directly, while staff learn shift assignments through messages. As locations increase, the owner has no single operational view of expected customers, staff coverage, booking changes, or who made a change. This creates avoidable coordination work and makes later audit difficult.

Mland needs a practical way to standardise booking and shift operations before opening another location. The immediate opportunity is not to replace every business system, but to establish one reliable source of operational truth for customer reservations, staff schedules, and traceable day-to-day changes across locations.
