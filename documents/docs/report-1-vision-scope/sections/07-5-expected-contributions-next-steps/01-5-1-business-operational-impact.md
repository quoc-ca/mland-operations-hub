## 5.1 Business & Operational Impact

**Target — validate with Mland before approval.** MOH should establish a baseline for booking volume, booking-confirmation time, manual coordination effort, cancellation/no-show rate, and staffing coverage before success targets are committed. A single multi-location view is expected to help the owner identify under-covered sessions and review changes without asking each location to reconstruct its records manually.
