#### b. Screen List

| # | Screen Name | Feature | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Home Page | Public Store Information | Introduces Mland's workshops, ready-made rings and custom design services, with entry points to sign-in, products, workshops, vouchers and reviews. A signed-in Member also gets the Member menu here. |

| 3 | User Register | Authentication | Allows visitors to create a Member account by email registration or Google SSO. |

| 5 | User Profile | Account Management | Allows a signed-in Member, Staff, Manager or Admin to view and edit their profile information. |

| 7 | Address Book | Account Management | Allows a signed-in user to add, edit and delete delivery addresses and choose a default one. |
| 8 | Product Catalogue | Product Management | Allows visitors and Members to browse published products, search by name, filter by category, price and stock, sort results, see each card's price after the best automatic discount and voucher, and add a variant to the cart from a Quick add box. |
| 9 | Product Details | Product Management | Displays product images, sizes with available quantity, applied and discounted prices (recalculated for the chosen branch), applicable offers, the size conversion chart and reviews; allows choosing size, branch and quantity to add to the cart or buy now. |

| 12 | AI Support Chat | AI Chatbox Analysis and Suggestion | Allows visitors and Members to ask the AI assistant general questions within a daily message limit (3 for a Guest, 10 for a Member). |
| 13 | Workshop Package List | Workshop Booking | Displays the published workshop packages for browsing and selection. |
| 14 | Workshop Package Details | Workshop Booking | Displays a package's description, terms, branches and sessions, with a way to start booking. |
| 15 | Session and Participant Booking | Workshop Booking | Allows a Guest or Member to choose the branch, session and number of participants, enter contact details and place a booking hold. |
| 16 | Ring Design Entry | Workshop Ring Design | Starts the ring designer for the selected workshop package from a ring model or a blank ring; a Guest may only preview. |
| 17 | Ring Model Selection | Workshop Ring Design | Allows visitors and Members to browse the base ring models and open one in the designer. |
| 18 | Ring Configuration | Workshop Ring Design | Allows a Member to configure and save the supported ring options, such as material and pattern, on a chosen model or a blank ring; a Guest may only preview and is sent to sign-in on save. |

| 20 | Design Evaluation | Workshop Ring Design | Displays a Member the evaluation of their saved design with recommendations and a path to a consultation. |
| 21 | Workshop Booking Cart | Workshop Booking | Allows the customer to review held workshop bookings, apply eligible benefits and go to the deposit payment. |
| 22 | Workshop Payment Result | Payment | Displays the system-verified payment outcome and the current status of the booking group after the return from VNPay; the browser return alone does not confirm payment. |
| 23 | Booking Lookup | Workshop Booking | Allows a Member to open their own booking groups, or a Guest to find one by booking code and contact, and manage it. |
| 24 | Booking Access (Magic Link) | Workshop Booking | Opens a read-only view of a booking group from the link sent by email. |
| 25 | Customer Folio | Workshop Settlement | Displays the charges, payments and balance of one workshop ticket to its buyer. |
| 26 | Mland Remedy | Workshop Settlement | Allows the buyer to respond to a session cancelled by Mland by moving to an equivalent session or choosing a refund. |
| 27 | Refund Account | Workshop Settlement | Allows a refund recipient to enter their bank account through a single-use link sent by email. |
| 28 | Public Review List | Review Management | Lists public reviews with target and rating filters, masked names and rating aggregates calculated from public reviews only. |
| 29 | Review Details | Review Management | Displays one public review with the current Manager reply, if any. |
| 30 | Guest Review Lookup (Retired) | Review Management | Retired: the Guest lookup by email or phone is removed; Guests only read reviews. |
| 31 | Create Review | Review Management | Collects from a Member a 1–5 rating and optional text up to 2,000 characters for a checked-in workshop or a picked-up or delivered retail item; a valid review is public at once. |
| 32 | Shopping Cart | Order Management and Fulfillment | Allows Members to review their own cart, update variant quantities, remove items and apply vouchers; prices remain indicative until checkout. |
| 33 | Retail Checkout | Order Management and Fulfillment | Allows Members to choose GHTK delivery or branch pickup, the address and the payment method (VNPay or cash at the counter), review the payable amount and place the order. |
| 34 | My Retail Orders | Order Management and Fulfillment | Allows Members to follow their retail orders by status tab, see each order's details, pay or retry a VNPay payment for a held order, and see the verified payment result after returning from VNPay. |
| 35 | My Loyalty | Loyalty and Promotion Management | Displays the Member's available, total and held points and the current point policy, with access to point history and saved vouchers. |
| 36 | Point History | Loyalty and Promotion Management | Allows Members to review their own point transactions (earned, held, spent, released and refunded) with their source and balance changes. |
| 37 | My Saved Vouchers | Loyalty and Promotion Management | Allows Members to review their saved vouchers by scope, separated into usable and expired or unusable, and go to products or workshops to use them. |
| 38 | My Ring Designs | Workshop Ring Design | Lists the workshop ring designs the Member has saved. |
| 39 | Design Request Workspace | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow | Allows a Member to describe a ring, upload reference images with quality feedback or attach a saved design, and submit a custom design request for human review. |
| 40 | Design Request Details | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow | Allows the owner to follow a design request's status and read the reviewer's written decision. |
| 41 | My Custom Orders | Custom Manufacturing | Lists the Member's custom manufacturing orders and their status. |
| 42 | Custom Order Details | Custom Manufacturing | Displays one custom order with its approved quote, the full-payment request and its fulfilment progress. |
| 43 | Consultation Chat | Staff Consultation Chat | Allows a Member to open a text consultation about one of their designs or a general topic, read its history, send messages and remove closed conversations. |
| 44 | Eligible Review Sources | Review Management | Shows a Member, on the review page, the sources still to be reviewed and the Member's own reviews. |
| 45 | Edit Own Review | Review Management | Allows the author to edit their own public review while recording a revision and audit entry. |
| 46 | Staff Home | Role Dashboards | Gathers the Staff member's daily workshop and order work in one place. |
| 47 | Product Management List | Product Management | Allows Staff and Managers to browse managed products by name or SKU, category and branch in counted status tabs (All, Drafts, Products to review, Rejected, Ready to publish, Published, Hidden, Prices to review) with a stock column; Managers can publish, hide, show again or open submissions for review, while Staff can stop or resume sale at their own branch and send price drafts or new prices for rejected ones. |
| 48 | New Product | Product Management | Allows Staff and Managers to enter product content, selling branches, sizes, prices, per-branch quantities and images; Staff prices stay proposals, Manager prices apply on creation. |
| 49 | Product Management Details | Product Management | Allows Staff and Managers to maintain content, images, branch quantities and sale state, add selling branches and new sizes, manage price proposals and price history, submit for initial review or delete while protecting holds; only Managers publish, hide or show the product again. |
| 50 | Price Proposal Form | Product Management | Allows Staff to propose new prices per size or for several sizes, save drafts and submit them for Manager review, and Managers to change the price of a size of a published product in one step (Change price); approved prices are applied as a separate step and submission never changes the applied price. |
| 51 | Product Categories | Product Management | Allows Staff and Managers to search categories by name, filter them by status, and create, edit, deactivate and reactivate product categories after reviewing affected products. |
| 52 | Import Products from Excel | Product Management | Allows Staff and Managers to download a branch-specific template, validate an .xlsx file, review row errors, import products as unpublished drafts and attach images. |
| 53 | Workshop Session List | Workshop Management | Displays today's and upcoming workshop sessions within the user's permitted scope for daily operations. |
| 54 | Session Details / Check-in | Workshop Management | Allows Staff and Managers to run one session: check in participants, change the session state, and finalize or cancel it. |
| 55 | Design Review Queue | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow | Lists the submitted design requests waiting for human review within the reviewer's scope. |
| 56 | Design Review Details | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow | Allows a Staff or Manager reviewer to inspect a design request and record a decision with the required written explanation. |
| 57 | Consultation Inbox | Staff Consultation Chat | Allows Staff (own location) and Managers (all locations) to claim, reply to, assign and close customer consultations. |
| 58 | Retail Order Operations | Order Management and Fulfillment | Allows branch Staff to start preparing, mark ready, record owner pickup, collect cash at the counter and record the GHTK handoff, without a manual paid override. |
| 59 | Review Moderation Queue | Review Management | Lists the public reviews that Staff and Managers can moderate. |

| 62 | Settlement Workspace | Workshop Settlement | Allows Staff and Managers to look up a ticket, take counter payments (cash or VNPay QR) and settle its balance. |
