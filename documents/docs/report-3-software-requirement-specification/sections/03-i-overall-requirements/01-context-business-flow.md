## 1. Context and Main Business Flow

Eight actors interact with the system: Guest, Member, Staff, Owner, AI API, Email Sender, Payment Gateway, and Admin Technical. A booking follows location/session selection, invoice creation, a 50% deposit, and confirmation only after a valid payment event. The workshop path may include check-in, auditable adjustments, settlement, and Staff-created continuation/custody. A Member may separately buy one ready ring after full confirmed payment, then choose pickup or carrier handoff.
