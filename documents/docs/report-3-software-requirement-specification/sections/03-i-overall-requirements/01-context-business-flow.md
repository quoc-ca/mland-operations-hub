## 1. Context Diagram

The Personalized Product Sales and Workshop Booking System is an operations platform for a ring atelier. It supports public discovery, workshop booking, workshop ring-design choices, retail sales, Member custom-manufacturing orders, and shop-side fulfilment. It is not a warehouse-management, ERP, accounting, payment-reconciliation, or end-to-end logistics system. The governing V1 scope and decision evidence are in [Report 2: Scope and Purpose](../../../report-2-project-management-plan/sections/02-i-project-overview/01-scope-purpose.md) and [Evidences](../../../report-2-project-management-plan/sections/02-i-project-overview/07-evidences.md).

> **Feature-004 update (2026-11-25):** The custom design request is Member-only, independent from the workshop booking flow and reviewed by people. AI image analysis and AI price suggestion are retired; basic AI text support remains.

The platform serves five primary roles. Guests browse products, workshop packages, schedules, ring models, and public store information; they may use basic AI support and create a workshop booking. Members additionally maintain profiles, place retail orders, use loyalty benefits, chat with Staff, submit a custom design request for human review, and create a separate custom-manufacturing order. Staff maintains the catalogue and available-to-sell quantities, checks in workshop participants, reviews independent Design Requests within the approved operational scope, prepares paid retail orders, and records pickup or GHTK handoff. Managers operate workshop schedules, staff assignments, capacity, materials, holiday exceptions, promotions, dashboards, review authority for independent Design Requests where required, and published prices. Admins manage accounts, fixed-role assignment, operational parameters, and approved integration credentials.

There are two deliberately separate customer journeys:

- **Custom design request.** A signed-in Member sends a description, reference images and/or a saved ring design, separately from workshop package, location, session, booking, payment, capacity and ticket flows. Staff or a Manager accepts, rejects or asks for clarification with a written explanation; no AI assesses the request. The owner can view the safe result. This flow does not create a booking or payment.
- **Custom manufacturing.** Only a Member creates a custom order, using a reference image or system-supported manual design/configuration, an optional requested deadline, and a fulfilment method. AI does not price, accept, reject, or review this order. Staff/Manager review precedes a Manager-approved quote. The Member pays the complete quote directly from the custom-order detail; no retail cart, deposit, or later balance exists. An unpaid quote expires/cancels and releases any temporary queue hold.

All online payment is through VNPay. Workshop and retail payment deadlines remain governed by their own contracts. A custom-order quote has one complete-payment deadline; no deposit or later balance payment is initiated. A signed VNPay IPN, or the defined signed QueryDR recovery result while the hold remains active, is required before the system applies a payment state change. The browser return URL is display-only. The system sends operational messages as responses within the relevant workflows; V1 does not introduce a notification centre.

Mland uses the paid Gemini API for basic text support only. Guest and Member basic support is limited to approved public information and FAQs; it cannot confirm transactions or make business decisions. Mland records text prompts/responses for no more than 30 days. Reference images of custom design requests and custom manufacturing require the applicable consent and follow their retention rules.

Klook is the sole V1 external workshop-registration partner. Mland remains the workshop-capacity source of truth: Klook obtains Mland availability/hold before confirming a booking, and Mland sends enabled availability or confirmed-booking updates to Klook. Klook cancellation events are not received or processed by Mland V1.

For retail and custom orders, the Member chooses store pickup or Giao Hang Tiet Kiem (GHTK) fulfilment after verified payment. Staff records the pickup or manual handoff to GHTK. The platform does not integrate with GHTK APIs, quote shipping fees, track delivery, handle delivery failure, returns, cancellations, or refunds. Recipient and address data for GHTK delivery are deleted or irreversibly obscured 30 days after handoff; VNPay transaction references and business audit evidence are retained for five years, and no card data is stored.
