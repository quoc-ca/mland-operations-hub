## 1. Context Diagram

The Personalized Product Sales and Workshop Booking System is an operations platform for a ring atelier. It supports public discovery, workshop booking, workshop ring-design choices, retail sales, Member custom-manufacturing orders, and shop-side fulfilment. It is not a warehouse-management, ERP, accounting, payment-reconciliation, or end-to-end logistics system. The governing V1 scope and decision evidence are in [Report 2: Scope and Purpose](../../../report-2-project-management-plan/sections/02-i-project-overview/01-scope-purpose.md) and [Evidences](../../../report-2-project-management-plan/sections/02-i-project-overview/07-evidences.md).

The platform serves five primary roles. Guests browse products, workshop packages, schedules, ring models, and public store information; they may use basic AI support and create a workshop booking. Members additionally maintain profiles, place retail orders, use loyalty benefits, chat with Staff, submit a booking-design image for AI analysis and review, and create a separate custom-manufacturing order. Staff maintains the catalogue and available-to-sell quantities, checks in workshop participants, reviews booking-design requests within the delegated threshold, prepares paid retail orders, and records pickup or GHTK handoff. Managers operate workshop schedules, staff assignments, capacity, materials, holiday exceptions, promotions, dashboards, and booking-design reviews above the delegated threshold; they also decide published prices after considering AI suggestions. Admins manage accounts, RBAC, operational parameters, and approved integration credentials.

There are two deliberately separate customer journeys:

- **Workshop booking and workshop ring design.** A customer selects a workshop package before choosing a supported design path. A Member may submit an image for AI component/material analysis as part of this booking journey. The resulting booking-design request is reviewed by Staff when its estimated value is at most 3,000,000 VND, otherwise by a Manager. The reviewer records the outcome and the Member can view it. This flow does not create a custom-manufacturing quotation.
- **Custom manufacturing.** Only a Member creates a custom order, using a reference image or system-supported manual design/configuration, an optional requested deadline, and a fulfilment method. AI does not price, accept, reject, or review this order. The system auto-accepts only when the deadline is valid and the custom-order queue has capacity. The custom order has its own 50% wax-package deposit and final-balance flow.

All online payment is through VNPay. A payment link expires after 10 minutes; a workshop slot, retail cart, or custom-order queue slot is held for at most 15 minutes. A signed VNPay IPN, or the defined signed QueryDR recovery result while the hold remains active, is required before the system applies a payment state change. The browser return URL is display-only. The system sends operational messages as responses within the relevant workflows; V1 does not introduce a notification centre.

Mland uses the paid Gemini API for basic support, booking-design image analysis, and product/package price suggestions. Gemini suggestions support human decisions only. Mland records text prompts/responses for no more than 30 days and does not retain AI image input after processing. Reference images for custom manufacturing are retained only until pickup or GHTK handoff; withdrawn or rejected custom requests and independently classified images are deleted immediately after processing.

Mland integrates with Google Maps Embed and Google Places on a read-only basis to show store locations and permitted public ratings/reviews with required attribution and a link to Google Maps. It does not persist Places review/rating content, collect internal reviews, or write reviews to Google. Klook is the sole V1 external workshop-registration partner. Mland remains the workshop-capacity source of truth: Klook obtains Mland availability/hold before confirming a booking, and Mland sends enabled availability or confirmed-booking updates to Klook. Klook cancellation events are not received or processed by Mland V1.

For retail and custom orders, the Member chooses store pickup or Giao Hang Tiet Kiem (GHTK) fulfilment after verified payment. Staff records the pickup or manual handoff to GHTK. The platform does not integrate with GHTK APIs, quote shipping fees, track delivery, handle delivery failure, returns, cancellations, or refunds. Recipient and address data for GHTK delivery are deleted or irreversibly obscured 30 days after handoff; VNPay transaction references and business audit evidence are retained for five years, and no card data is stored.
