### 4.1 Actors

| # | Actor | Description |
 --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Guest | An unauthenticated user visiting the platform. They can browse available in-stock products, view workshop package information (Ring Crafting, Clay Molding, Wax Carving), search or filter schedules by store branch locations and available materials, look up public customer reviews and store ratings, chat with the AI support assistant, book a workshop session, and register a new member account or log into the system. |
| 2 | Member | A registered customer of the platform. They can update basic personal profile information (changing passwords), enter their delivery address directly during the checkout step, manage their shopping cart, place orders for available in-stock jewelry products with an automatic stock deduction upon successful order creation (non-real-time mechanism), book workshop sessions, track purchase order history, engage in online chat support with staff or AI, and submit reviews and star ratings for completed products or workshop experiences. |
| 3 | Staff | A store employee responsible for daily operational execution. They assist customers via online chat for consultation requests (*); after receiving specific requirement details from the customer through the conversation, Staff will issue an official crafting price quote on the system. They also manage the product catalog alongside real-time inventory stock quantities, and perform check-ins for customers attending workshops in person at the store by scanning digital QR tickets or verifying manually. |
| 4 | Manager | A store operations manager. They directly manage workshop schedules, configure time slots, set material availability limits per store branch, establish seat capacities and holiday/off-day exceptions, manage promotion campaigns and voucher codes, review and approve high-value price quotes generated by Staff, and access executive dashboard overviews to monitor total system revenue. |
| 5 | Admin | The system administrator holding full administrative privileges over the platform. They configure core operational parameters (System_Parameters) such as AI pricing margins, material-based deposit percentages, currency settings, and VAT rates. They also manage all user accounts (creating, granting roles, locking or unlocking Staff, Manager, and Member accounts), configure role-based access control boundaries, and manage API keys and credentials for all third-party services. |
| 6 | SSO Provider | An external identity provider (such as Google or Facebook) integrated via Firebase Auth. It receives OAuth authentication requests from the system and returns verified authentication credentials along with user profile information (email, full name) to enable seamless registration and rapid login capabilities. |
| 7 | Mail Gateway | An external email service provider or system notification channel. It receives automated dispatch requests from the platform to deliver security OTP verification codes for user registration and password resets, as well as operational notifications including digital workshop QR tickets, payment e-receipts, design quote notifications, and order status updates to users. |
| 8 | AI Support / AI API | An external artificial intelligence service integrated into the platform. It operates an automated customer support chatbot to answer user inquiries, provide information, and assist in online customer service chats. |
| 9 | Payment Gateway | An external online payment service provider (such as VNPAY). It securely processes online payment transactions and deposits for workshop bookings, retail product purchases, and custom manufacturing orders, returning transaction status callbacks (Webhooks/IPN) to confirm order fulfillment or trigger ticket generation. |
| 10 | Google Maps API | An external mapping and review service[cite: 6, 7]. It provides location rendering and address mapping for physical store branches while automatically synchronizing store review and rating data from Google Maps to display publicly on the platform. |
| 11 | Cloud Storage Service | An external media storage provider (such as AWS S3 or Firebase Storage). It securely stores and serves media files for product catalog images and reference design photos. |
| 12 | Delivery Provider | A third-party logistics provider authorized to perform physical parcel delivery to customers. |
| 13 | External Registration System | An external third-party ticketing or experience platform (such as Klook or GetYourGuide). It exchanges two-way synchronization data with the platform regarding workshop seat availability, booking registrations, and time slots. |
