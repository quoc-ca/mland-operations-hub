### 4.1 Actors

| # | Actor | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Guest | An unauthenticated visitor who can browse the published product catalogue and available ring models, view workshop packages and schedules, view permitted public store information, use basic AI support, book a workshop, and register or sign in through the configured identity provider. A Guest selects a workshop package before a supported ring-design path. A Guest cannot submit a booking-design image, create a custom-manufacturing order, or complete retail checkout. |
| 2 | Member | An authenticated customer who can maintain a profile, manage a cart, place retail orders, book workshop sessions, view purchase history, use loyalty benefits, and use plain-text consultation chat with Staff or basic AI support. A Member may select an available ring model, configure a workshop ring from system-provided components, or submit a booking-design image for AI analysis and Staff/Manager review. Separately, a Member may create a custom-manufacturing order with a reference image or manual configuration, optional requested deadline, and fulfilment method. AI does not decide that custom order. After verified retail payment, the Member selects store pickup or GHTK fulfilment; GHTK requires recipient and delivery-address data for that order. |
| 3 | Staff | A store employee responsible for catalogue products, images, prices, publication, manually maintained available-to-sell quantities, workshop check-in by QR or manual verification, and independent plain-text consultation chat. Staff reviews a booking-design request with estimated value at most 3,000,000 VND and records its outcome. Staff prepares paid retail orders, records customer pickup, and records manual handoff to GHTK. Staff or Manager records the final amount due for a ready custom-manufacturing order. |
| 4 | Manager | A store operations manager who manages workshop schedules, Staff assignments, time slots, capacity, material availability, holiday exceptions, promotion campaigns, voucher codes, and the executive revenue dashboard. The Manager considers AI suggestions and decides published prices for new workshop packages or retail products. A booking-design request with estimated value above 3,000,000 VND is reviewed by the Manager. The Manager may configure the custom-order queue and record final custom-order amounts. |
| 5 | Admin | The system administrator who manages user accounts, roles, account lock status, role-based access control, operational parameters, and approved third-party integration credentials. Operational parameters include the custom-order minimum deadline, unavailable deadline dates, and maximum queue size. Application account status and authorization are maintained in the system database. |
| 6 | SSO Provider | An external identity service used for registration, login, password recovery, and issuing identity tokens. Provider establishes identity only; the platform resolves account status, business roles, and authorization from its own database. |
| 7 | Mail Gateway | An external Mland-domain SMTP service that delivers platform-requested account-security messages and workflow responses, including workshop QR tickets, payment requests/receipts, booking-design review results, and order-status messages. It does not make business decisions or provide a notification centre. |
| 8 | AI API | The paid Gemini API, used only for basic text support, booking-design image component/material analysis, and price suggestions for new workshop packages or retail products. Its suggestions support a human decision only; it does not price, accept, reject, or review custom-manufacturing orders. |
| 9 | Payment Gateway | VNPay, the sole V1 payment provider. It processes workshop deposits, retail payments, and custom-manufacturing deposits/balances. A signed IPN, or signed QueryDR recovery while the hold remains active, confirms a payment after signature, reference, amount, and success-status validation; a Return URL does not confirm payment. |
| 10 | Google Maps API | Google Maps Embed and Google Places, used read-only to render store locations and permitted public review/rating information with required attribution and a link back to Google Maps. Mland neither persists Places review/rating content nor writes reviews to Google. |
| 11 | Cloud Storage Service | An external media store for catalogue assets and custom-manufacturing reference images. Custom-manufacturing images are retained only until pickup or GHTK handoff; rejected or withdrawn requests and independently classified images are deleted immediately after processing. |
| 12 | Delivery Provider | Giao Hang Tiet Kiem (GHTK), used only for Staff-recorded manual handoff of paid retail or custom orders. Mland does not use GHTK APIs, quote delivery fees, track delivery, or process delivery failure, return, cancellation, or refund workflows. |
| 13 | External Registration System | Klook, the sole V1 external workshop-registration partner. It obtains Mland availability/hold before confirming a Klook booking and receives enabled Mland availability or confirmed-booking updates. Klook cancellation events are not received or processed by Mland V1. |
