### 4.1 Actors

::: {.docx-table width="100%" columns="15%,25%,60%" repeat-header="false"}

| # | Actor | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Guest | An unauthenticated visitor who can browse the published product catalogue and available ring models, view workshop packages and schedules, view permitted public store information, use basic AI support, book a workshop, and register or sign in through the configured identity provider. A Guest may see the custom design request and ring-design entry points and public options, but cannot create, upload, track, or read a Design Request or ring design; a protected action prompts sign-in (Feature-004 and Feature-012 member-only rebaseline, 2026-11-24). A Guest may browse public reviews but cannot create one. A Guest cannot create a custom-manufacturing order or complete retail checkout. |
| 2 | Member | An authenticated customer who can maintain a profile, manage a cart, place retail orders, book workshop sessions, view purchase history, use loyalty benefits, and use plain-text consultation chat with Staff or basic AI support. A Member may create an independent Design Request for human review under Feature-004 ownership, consent, and review policy, and may create or edit reviews for eligible workshop attendances and retail order lines. Separately, a Member may create a custom-manufacturing order with a reference image or manual configuration, optional requested deadline, and fulfilment method. AI does not decide that custom order. After verified retail payment, the Member selects store pickup or GHTK fulfilment; GHTK requires recipient and delivery-address data for that order. |
| 3 | Staff | A store employee responsible for catalogue products, images, prices, publication, manually maintained available-to-sell quantities, workshop check-in by QR or manual verification, and plain-text consultation chat within the Staff member's current Workshop Location scope. Staff may review an independent Design Request only within the approved Feature-004 operational scope and records its outcome. Staff prepares paid retail orders, records customer pickup, and records manual handoff to GHTK. Staff or Manager prepares the quote of a custom order and records its production and handover. |
| 4 | Manager | A store operations manager with global operational scope for an action explicitly authorized to Manager; a Workshop Location assignment is organizational and does not narrow that scope. The Manager manages workshop schedules, Staff assignments, time slots, capacity, material availability, holiday exceptions, promotion campaigns, voucher codes, and the executive revenue dashboard. The Manager decides published prices for new workshop packages or retail products. The Manager reviews an independent Design Request when the approved Feature-004 review policy requires Manager authority, and may claim, assign, respond to, close or reopen consultation conversations globally. The Manager may approve custom-order quotes. Admin-only, ownership-only, and public actions remain governed by their explicit rule. |
| 5 | Admin | The system administrator who manages user accounts, fixed-role assignment, account lock status, operational parameters, and approved third-party integration credentials. Custom-order queue parameters are retired from V1 and are not part of the Admin scope. Application account status and authorization are maintained in the system database. |
| 6 | SSO Provider | An external identity service used for registration, login, password recovery, and issuing identity tokens. Provider establishes identity only; the platform resolves account status, business roles, and authorization from its own database. |
| 7 | Mail Gateway | An external Mland-domain SMTP service that delivers mandatory Staff/Manager invitations and approved business messages, including workshop QR tickets and retail order updates. Firebase sends Member verification and password-recovery emails. SMTP does not make business decisions or provide a notification centre. |
| 8 | AI API | The paid Gemini API, used only for basic text support. It does not receive Feature-004 images, analyze design images, suggest prices, or price, accept, reject, or review design requests and custom-manufacturing orders. |
| 9 | Payment Gateway | VNPay, the sole V1 payment provider. It processes workshop deposits, retail payments, and one-time custom-manufacturing quote payments. A signed IPN, or signed QueryDR recovery while the hold remains active, confirms a payment after signature, reference, amount, and success-status validation; a Return URL does not confirm payment. |
| 10 | Google Maps API (Retired) | No longer used: the platform shows no Google Maps or Places content and opens no Google review link (confirmed 2026-11-25). |
| 11 | Cloud Storage Service | An external media store for catalogue assets and custom-manufacturing reference images. Custom-manufacturing images are retained only until pickup or GHTK handoff; images of rejected or withdrawn requests are deleted immediately after processing. |
| 12 | Delivery Provider | Giao Hang Tiet Kiem (GHTK). Mland calls GHTK only to quote the delivery fee; the GHTK partner account records courier pickup and delivery in the platform. There is no provider shipment, tracking, delivery-failure, return, cancellation or refund integration. |
| 13 | External Registration System | An OTA partner (Klook and GetYourGuide are candidates) that books against Mland availability and sends booking and cancellation events. Mland remains the capacity and ticket authority; only a confirmed partner cancellation releases them. No partner is enabled before its go-live gates are evidenced. |

:::
