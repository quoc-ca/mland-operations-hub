### 4.1 Actors

::: {.docx-table width="100%" columns="15%,25%,60%" repeat-header="false"}

| # | Actor | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Guest | An unauthenticated visitor who can browse the published product catalogue, workshop packages and sessions, public promotions and public reviews, use basic AI chat support, and can register an account. A Guest can book a workshop and pay its deposit through VNPay without member benefits, look up the booking by group code or Magic Link, and send a ticket to another participant. A Guest may open the design request and ring configurator pages for a local, unsaved preview only. A Guest cannot complete retail checkout, save a voucher, write a review, save a ring design, create a design request or use consultation chat; each protected action prompts sign-in. |
| 2 | Member | An authenticated customer who maintains a profile, email, password and shipping address book. A Member can use the cart, check out at a chosen branch with store pickup or GHTK delivery, pay through VNPay or cash at the counter (pickup only), and follow order and shipment status. A Member can book workshops with benefits, cancel a booking or selected tickets, request session changes, save and use vouchers, redeem loyalty points and view My Loyalty. A Member can configure, save, archive and restore ring designs, submit and revise a custom design request, view its quote and pay the full quote through VNPay. A Member can write and edit one review per eligible source and use plain-text consultation chat with Staff or Manager or basic AI chat support. |
| 3 | Staff | A branch employee who works only within the single active Workshop Location assigned to the account. Staff creates draft products, uploads product images, proposes prices, imports products from Excel, applies approved prices and maintains branch stock; only a Manager publishes or hides a product. Staff checks in workshop participants by QR code or manual lookup for sessions assigned to them, resends tickets, finalizes attendance, settles folios and collects balances at the counter. Staff processes paid retail orders at the branch, confirms cash payment, records customer pickup and marks orders ready for the carrier. Staff reviews custom design requests with an explanation for each decision and prepares the quote inputs when assigned, records the production and handover of paid custom orders, hides published reviews, and answers consultation chats in the branch scope. |
| 4 | Manager | A store operations manager. The Manager configures workshop locations, slots, sessions, package availability and Staff assignments, cancels sessions, reverses check-ins and handles late payments, refunds and ticket changes. The Manager approves, adjusts or rejects product and price proposals, publishes or hides products and manages categories. The Manager manages promotion campaigns, vouchers and the loyalty point policy, manages email templates and delivery, and reviews custom design requests and approves their quotes. The Manager hides, removes and replies to reviews, claims and assigns consultation chats, and views the dashboard and payment reconciliation. Manager scope is global for workshop, product, notification and chat actions; retail order actions apply only to delegated branches, and the dashboard shows only assigned branches. |
| 5 | Admin | The system administrator. The Admin creates Staff, Manager and Carrier accounts, sends invitations, edits, suspends and restores accounts, and assigns roles and Workshop Locations. The Admin maintains approved system and operational parameters, may resend an email whose delivery outcome is unknown and may retrieve a failed invitation manually. The Admin has no business authority over products, workshops, promotions, orders, design requests, reviews or chat. |
| 6 | Carrier (GHTK Partner) | A GHTK partner account created by the Admin, with no Workshop Location. Through the carrier portal the Carrier sees GHTK shipments of every branch with the pickup branch, recipient, address, item count and fee, but no prices, vouchers or Member data. The Carrier records only shipment pickup and successful delivery, and views delivery statistics by day, week, month or year. |
| 7 | Mail Gateway (SMTP) | An external Mland-domain SMTP service that delivers emails requested by the platform: internal account invitations, workshop booking confirmations with QR tickets, and retail order confirmations. Firebase verification and password-reset emails are not sent through it. It makes no business decisions. |
| 8 | Cloud Storage Service (Cloudinary) | An external media store for product images and other uploaded files, such as transfer-receipt images, which are stored as authenticated objects. |
| 9 | SSO Provider (Firebase Authentication) | An external identity service for email/password and Google sign-in, email verification, password reset, email change, and Carrier identities. It establishes identity only; the platform resolves account status, role, location and authorization from its own database. |
| 10 | Payment Gateway (VNPay) | The online payment provider for workshop deposits, retail prepaid checkout and custom design quotes. Only a verified, signed VNPay result, or a signed QueryDR status check, changes a payment state; the browser return page does not. Refunds are manual bank transfers approved by a Manager, not VNPay refunds. |
| 11 | Delivery Provider (GHTK) | Giao Hang Tiet Kiem, used for retail delivery. The platform only requests a shipping fee quote (50 g per ring); it does not create shipments or receive status updates from GHTK, and the shipment record and its courier steps are kept in the platform. Cash on delivery is not used. When GHTK is not available, only store pickup is offered. |
| 12 | AI API (Gemini) | The paid Gemini API, used only for basic AI chat support for Guests and Members. The platform sends the chat request and returns the response. Answers are limited to approved public information and FAQs; the AI cannot confirm transactions or make business decisions. It does not receive design-request images, analyze designs, suggest prices, or review design requests or custom orders. |

:::
