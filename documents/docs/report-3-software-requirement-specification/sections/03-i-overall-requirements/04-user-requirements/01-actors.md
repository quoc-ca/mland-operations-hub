### 4.1 Actors

| # | Actor | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Guest | An unauthenticated visitor who can browse product catalogue and available ring models, view workshop packages, search or filter workshop schedules, view public reviews and ratings, use the basic AI support chat, book a workshop, and register or sign in through Firebase Authentication. During a workshop journey, a Guest selects a slot before selecting a supported ring-design path. Guest cannot submit an image-based custom design request or complete retail checkout. |
| 2 | Member | An authenticated customer who can maintain basic profile information, manage a cart, place retail orders, book workshop sessions, view order history, submit reviews, and use plain-text consultation chat with Staff or the basic AI support chat. A Member may choose an existing model, configure a ring from system-provided components, or submit an image-based custom design request. The image is submitted only through that request, never through chat. Each custom request enters `need_review` and ends as `accepted` or `rejected`; a rejected decision includes a reason. A completed purchase design proceeds to the cart; a booking design follows slot selection. After successful retail payment, the Member chooses store pickup or carrier fulfilment; carrier fulfilment requires delivery contact and address details. |
| 3 | Staff | A store employee responsible for product catalogue and available-to-sell quantity maintenance, workshop check-in by QR or manual verification, and independent plain-text consultation chat with customers. Staff reviews a `need_review` design request that needs difficulty review when its estimated price is at most 3,000,000 VND, then accepts or rejects it. A rejection must include a reason. Staff does not chat as part of the custom design request lifecycle. |
| 4 | Manager | A store operations manager who manages workshop schedules, time slots, capacity, material availability, holiday exceptions, promotion campaigns and voucher codes, and views the executive revenue dashboard. The Manager may view AI package-price suggestions. A `need_review` design request with estimated price above 3,000,000 VND is reviewed by the Manager regardless of its difficulty result; a Manager rejection must include a reason. |
| 5 | Admin | The system administrator who configures operational parameters, including AI pricing parameters, material-based deposit percentages, currency and VAT settings; manages user accounts, roles, account lock status and role-based access control; and manages third-party API credentials. Application account status and role authorization are maintained in the system database. |
| 6 | SSO Provider | An external identity service used for registration, login, password recovery, and issuing identity tokens. Provider establishes identity only; the platform resolves account status, business roles, and authorization from its own database. |
| 7 | Mail Gateway | An external email or notification channel that delivers platform-requested account-security messages and operational notifications, including workshop QR tickets, payment receipts, design quote notifications, and order-status updates. It does not make business decisions. |
| 8 | AI API | An external AI service used only to provide basic text support to Guests and Members, classify components in a Member-submitted custom design image, and provide package-price suggestions to Managers. It does not estimate custom-design price or difficulty and does not chat after a custom design request is submitted. |
| 9 | Payment Gateway | An external provider that processes workshop deposits, retail payments, and custom manufacturing order payments, then returns transaction callbacks. Payment confirmation is a system flow, not a business authorization or actor-goal use case. |
| 10 | Google Maps API | An external mapping and review service that renders store locations and synchronizes public store review and rating data for display. |
| 11 | Cloud Storage Service | An external media store for product catalogue images and Member-submitted custom design reference images. |
| 12 | Delivery Provider | A third-party provider that physically delivers paid retail orders selected for carrier fulfilment. The platform records only whether the order is prepared or packed for carrier; it does not track delivery progress. |
| 13 | External Registration System | An external ticketing or experience platform, such as Klook or GetYourGuide. It can call platform APIs to synchronize workshop schedules and bookings, and it can receive schedule or booking updates initiated by the platform. |
