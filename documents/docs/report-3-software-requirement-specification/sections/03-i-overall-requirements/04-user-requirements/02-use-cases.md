### 4.2 Use Cases (UC)

::: {.docx-table width="100%" columns="10%,15%,25%,50%" repeat-header="false"}

|  ID | Use Case                                | Feature                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Use Case Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --: | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | Register Account                        | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Creates a Member account after successful identity registration via email or SSO.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|   2 | Sign In                                 | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Authenticates the user and resolves the corresponding platform account and role-based permissions.                                                                                                                                                                    |
|   3 | Reset Password                          | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Resets a user's password through the configured email-based recovery flow.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|   4 | Change Password                         | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Reauthenticates an eligible password-account owner and changes their password through Firebase.                                                                                                                                                                        |
|   5 | Manage Personal Profile                 | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Allows the authenticated user to view and update permitted personal profile information, including name, phone number, and address.                                                                                                                                   |
|   6 | Manage User Account                     | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Creates, views, or updates internal platform staff and user accounts.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|   7 | Assign User Role                        | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Assigns a business operational role stored in the platform database to an account.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|   8 | Lock or Unlock User Account             | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Changes whether a user account is active or restricted from accessing the platform.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 10 | Browse and View Products | Product Management | Guest or Member searches, filters, sorts and pages through published products, opens a product's images, applied price, available quantity per size and public reviews, and selects a size, branch and quantity to add to the cart or buy now, from the product page or a Quick add box on the list. Sizes that are stopped from sale or out of stock stay visible but cannot be selected; signed-in non-Member roles see no cart buttons. |
| 12 | Manage Products | Product Management | Staff or Manager creates draft products with 1–25 sizes (5–29) and system-generated SKUs, manually or by importing an `.xlsx` template with an all-or-nothing preview, adds sizes later at a selling branch, updates content and images, manages branch stock (adds selling branches, sets quantity per size at each branch, stops or resumes sale at a branch), stops or resumes the sale of a size, and soft-deletes a product. Staff changes stock only at assigned active branches; at an inactive branch only a Manager can change the quantity. |
|  14 | Submit Product or Price Proposal        | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff submits a new product, and Staff or Manager submits a new price for an existing size, for Manager approval; the submitter's branches at submission are recorded. Each size has at most one pending price proposal at a time.                                    |
| 15 | Approve and Publish Product | Product Management | The Manager accepts, adjusts, or rejects with a reason a pending product or price proposal, seeing who submitted it, from which branches, and who decided it. An approved price is then applied as a separate step by Staff or Manager, affecting only new views and checkouts. The Manager publishes a product that meets all conditions, or hides it from the public catalogue while keeping it in product management. |
|  18 | Manage Product Categories               | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff or Manager finds categories by status and name, creates, renames, activates, or deactivates product categories with system-wide unique names, and reviews the affected products before deactivation.                                                            |
|  19 | Browse Workshop Packages                | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Displays available workshop packages with pricing, durations, and overview details.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|  20 | View Workshop Package Details           | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Displays comprehensive package specifications, included materials, tools, and terms.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|  21 | Create Workshop Booking                 | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Puts one or more packages with their session sets and participant counts into a cart and confirms it, creating a booking group with one ticket per person and holding the seats for 12 minutes. |
|  22 | Pay Workshop Deposit                    | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Pays the workshop booking group through VNPay: each ticket collects its final price up to 200,000 VND. The booking is confirmed after a valid payment confirmation, or without VNPay when the group total is below 5,000 VND. |
|  23 | Look Up Booking Details                 | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Opens a booking group: a Member through sign-in, a Guest with the group code and one stored contact email or phone, or through the emailed Magic Link; shows tickets, status, amounts and, for the buyer, QR codes. |
|  24 | Check In Participant                    | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Confirms attendee presence at the workshop via QR code scanning or manual code lookup.                                                                                                                                                                                |
|  25 | Manage Workshop Schedule                | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Manager creates sessions singly or in bulk, publishes, pauses or cancels them, and changes their capacity; date, slot and location of an existing session cannot be edited. |
|  26 | Assign Staff to Workshop Slot           | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Manager revokes or restores Staff assignments of a session; active in-scope Staff are assigned automatically. |
|  27 | Configure Workshop Time Slot            | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Defines the standard daily operating timeframes for workshop sessions.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|  28 | Configure Workshop Slot Capacity        | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Sets the maximum seat capacity and participant limits for specific workshop slots.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|  29 | Configure Supported Materials by Branch (Retired) | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Retired: material configuration by branch is not part of the platform. Branch availability is managed per workshop package. |
|  30 | Configure Holiday and Off-Day Exception (Retired) | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Retired: holiday and off-day exceptions are not part of the platform. Managers create, pause or cancel individual workshop sessions. |
| 31 | Synchronize Workshop Schedule (Retired) | Workshop Management | Retired: external partner synchronization is not part of the platform (Stakeholder statement, Feature-003 owner, 2026-11-25). |
| 32 | Synchronize Workshop Booking (Retired) | Workshop Management | Retired: external partner synchronization is not part of the platform (Stakeholder statement, Feature-003 owner, 2026-11-25). |
|  33 | Chat with Basic AI Support              | AI Chatbox Analysis and Suggestion                                                                                                                                                                                          | Answers customer natural language inquiries regarding products, workshops, and store FAQs.                                                                                                                                                                            |
|  36 | Select Available Ring Model             | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Chooses a pre-designed, ready-to-make ring template available in the system catalog.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|  37 | Configure Workshop Ring                 | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | A signed-in Member configures the ring to be made in a workshop by assembling compatible system-provided bands, gems, and engravings; a Guest may only browse the public options.                                                                                                                                                    |
|  38 | Chat with Staff Consultant              | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Initiates authenticated plain-text consultation polling with Staff or Manager using an owned workshop-ring design or independent Design Request context. Staff is location-scoped, Manager is global, and chat does not approve designs, publish prices, or mutate booking/payment/order state.                                                                                                                                                                 |
|  39 | Submit Custom Design Request             | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | A signed-in Member submits a description, up to five reference images, and/or a saved Feature-012 ring design as a custom design request for human review. It is not attached to a workshop booking; a Guest is prompted to sign in.                                                                                                                                   |
|  40 | Review Custom Design Request            | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Staff or Manager reviews an independent Design Request according to the approved Feature-004 authority policy and records the outcome.                                                                                                                                |
|  41 | View Custom Design Request Decision     | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Allows the authorized owner to view the safe recorded outcome of the reviewed independent Design Request.                                                                                                                                                             |
| 42 | View Loyalty Points | Loyalty and Promotion Management | Displays the Member's own point balance, held points, available points (balance minus held points), current point value, and a chronological history of earned, redeemed, released, and refunded points linked to each order or booking. Points do not expire and there are no membership tiers. |
| 44 | Apply Promotions and Points | Loyalty and Promotion Management | At retail checkout or workshop booking, the system applies the best automatic discount, the Member may add at most one voucher and choose points up to the configured cap, and the system shows the exact total. A Guest booking directly receives only the workshop automatic discount. Benefits are held on acceptance and finalized after verified payment; points are then earned, and held or used benefits released or refunded, automatically from the order or booking outcome. |
| 45 | Manage Promotions | Loyalty and Promotion Management | The Manager creates, edits, pauses, or resumes code-free automatic discounts and vouchers with a system-generated code for products or workshop packages, setting scope, discount, cap, minimum spend, limits, point combination and distribution, and tracks usage. When several automatic discounts apply, the largest is used. |
|  47 | View and Save Vouchers                  | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | Guest or Member views voucher cards and their conditions on product and package pages and in the Voucher hub, which lists only vouchers that must be saved; a signed-in Member saves codes to My vouchers. Fully claimed vouchers stay visible but cannot be saved, and saving a voucher that must be saved claims one of its uses for that Member, while saving a Public voucher reserves nothing; saving never guarantees eligibility at checkout. Product and workshop package pages show the price after the largest automatic discount and then the best voucher for one item or ticket, labelled "Price after voucher {code}". |
|  51 | Manage Loyalty Point Policy             | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The Manager configures the earning rate, point value, minimum points per use, and point-discount cap. Changes apply only to transactions accepted afterwards.                                                                                                         |
|  52 | Add Product to Cart                     | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Adds a selected retail product with chosen variant options (size, engraving) into the cart.                                                                                                                                                                           |
|  53 | Manage Shopping Cart                    | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays current cart contents, updates item quantities, and removes items from the cart.                                                                                                                                                                             |
|  54 | Checkout Retail Order                   | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Revalidates the ticked cart lines, snapshots prices, applies discounts, voucher and points, creates one order per branch (all or none) and holds the goods for 10 minutes (VNPay) or 72 hours (cash at the counter). |
|  55 | Pay for Retail Order                    | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Pays the full retail order through VNPay. A valid signed VNPay confirmation, rather than the browser Return URL, completes the sale and deducts held quantities.                                                                                                      |
|  56 | View Purchase Order History             | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays a Member's complete chronological list of past and ongoing retail orders.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|  57 | View Order Details                      | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays payment, fulfilment and recorded GHTK shipment steps of a retail order; there is no GHTK provider tracking. |
|  58 | Review & Confirm Retail Order           | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Staff reviews a paid order, which is already being prepared, packs it and marks it ready. |
|  59 | Fulfill Retail Order                    | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | After verified payment, records on each paid order the pickup or GHTK choice made at checkout (with one shipment record for a GHTK order) and shows its fulfilment progress. |
|  60 | Record Customer Pickup                  | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Staff records the handover of a ready pickup order, collecting counter cash where it is due. |
|  61 | Mark Order as Prepared for Carrier      | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Staff marks a prepared GHTK order ready for the courier; the GHTK partner account records the courier pickup and the delivery. |
|  62 | Configure System Operational Parameters | Administrative Parameter Configuration | Admin configures operational values and the proposed workshop-ring difficulty policy. Custom-order queue parameters are retired from V1. |
| 63 | Manage Third-Party API Credentials | Administrative Parameter Configuration | Maintains approved credentials and endpoints for VNPay, Gemini, Mland SMTP, and cloud storage. |
|  64 | View Dashboard                          | Administrative Parameter Configuration                                                                                                                                                                                      | Visualizes consolidated business analytics, workshop utilization, retail revenue, branch KPIs and best sellers.                                                                                                                                                                 |
|  67 | Submit Custom Order                     | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Turns a Member's accepted custom design request into a custom order: Staff or a Manager prepares the quote and a Manager approves it. |
|  69 | Pay Custom Order Quote                  | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Pays the complete Manager-approved custom-order quote through VNPay within a 10-minute payment session. No retail cart or deposit is used; the quote remains payable until its quote deadline. |
|  70 | View Custom Order                       | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Allows the Member to view the custom-manufacturing order, production timeline, and GHTK delivery tracking details.                                                                                                                                                     |
|  71 | Record Custom Payment Outcome           | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Records the verified full-payment outcome or the expired/cancelled outcome for the active custom-order quote. No final balance is created. |
|  72 | Fulfil Paid Custom Order                | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Allows production and fulfilment only after the complete quoted amount is verified. |
|  73 | Fulfil Custom Order                     | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Staff packs the finished custom item and advances shipment; the GHTK courier records pickup and delivery, automatically closing the order.                                                                                                                            |
|  74 | Browse Public Reviews                   | Review Management                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Guest or Member browses published workshop-session and retail-product reviews and aggregates. Creating or editing a review requires sign-in.                                                                                                                          |
|  75 | Create or Edit Own Review               | Review Management                                                                                                                                                                                                           | A signed-in Member creates or edits a review for an eligible source: a checked-in workshop attendance, or a retail order line that was picked up or delivered. Each source has at most one review.                                                                    |
|  76 | Moderate Review                         | Review Management                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Staff hides a published review; Manager hides, removes, or replies to one. Admin has technical/audit access only.                                                                                                                                                     |
|  77 | View Ticket Folio                       | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The ticket owner (Member, or Guest after contact proof), assigned Staff, or Manager views the bill, add-ons and remaining balance of a workshop ticket. |
|  78 | Add Folio Charge                        | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Assigned Staff or Manager adds a customer-consented add-on or variable charge to an open folio after the session starts. |
|  79 | Collect Ticket Balance                  | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Assigned Staff or Manager collects the remaining ticket balance by VNPay, cash or bank transfer, paid by the Guest or Member; the folio is finalized when the balance reaches zero. |
|  80 | Verify Transfer Payment                 | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Manager confirms a pending bank-transfer collection or marks a pending collection as failed. |
|  81 | Manage Handover Exception and Debt      | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Manager approves handover before payment verification and records or closes the resulting debt. |
|  82 | Correct Ticket Folio                    | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Manager voids a folio line or collection, or corrects a finalized overcharge. Reversal of closed-day cash is specified but not yet built. |
|  83 | Close Branch Cash Day                   | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff of the branch, or Manager, submits the daily cash count; a Manager resolves any difference. |
|  84 | Cancel Workshop Tickets                 | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The buyer (Member, or Guest after one-time-code proof) cancels one or more unused tickets, all or none; Staff or Manager may act on the buyer's behalf with the buyer proof. |
|  85 | Change Ticket Sessions                  | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The buyer (Guest or Member), assigned Staff or Manager selects valid replacement sessions for a ticket. Changing to another location is still marked as draft in the Feature-003.3 spec. |
|  86 | Decide Ticket Change Request            | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff of the location, or Manager, approves or rejects a queued ticket change request. |
|  87 | Grant Extra Change Allowance            | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Manager grants one extra counted change to a ticket. |
|  88 | Respond to Mland Cancellation Offer     | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | When Mland cancels, the Guest or Member buyer chooses a replacement session or a refund; Staff or Manager may submit that choice at the counter with the buyer proof. |
|  89 | Submit Refund Account                   | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The Guest or Member payer enters and confirms the receiving account through a single-use link; Staff or Manager may enter it at the counter. |
|  90 | Process Refund Request                  | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Manager approves, records the transfer, confirms, refuses, resends the link for, or closes and reopens a refund request. |
