### 4.2 Use Cases (UC)

::: {.docx-table width="100%" columns="10%,15%,25%,50%" repeat-header="false"}

|  ID | Use Case                                | Feature                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Use Case Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| --: | --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | Register Account                        | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Creates a Member account after successful identity registration via email or SSO.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|   2 | Sign In                                 | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Authenticates the user and resolves the corresponding platform account and role-based permissions.                                                                                                                                                                    |
|   3 | Reset Password                          | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Resets a user's password through the configured email-based recovery flow.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|   4 | Change Password                         | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Changes the authenticated user's password and triggers an account-security notification.                                                                                                                                                                              |
|   5 | Manage Personal Profile                 | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Allows the authenticated user to view and update permitted personal profile information, including name, phone number, and address.                                                                                                                                   |
|   6 | Manage User Account                     | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Creates, views, or updates internal platform staff and user accounts.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|   7 | Assign User Role                        | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Assigns a business operational role stored in the platform database to an account.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|   8 | Lock or Unlock User Account             | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Changes whether a user account is active or restricted from accessing the platform.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|   9 | Configure Role-Based Access Control     | Account Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Maintains granular permissions and access policies for each platform role.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|  10 | Browse Product Catalogue                | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Displays published, non-deleted products in active categories. Guest or Member searches by keyword, filters by category, price range and purchasable status, sorts the results, and pages through 20 products per page. A product card can open a Quick add box to add a size at a branch to the cart without leaving the list. |
|  11 | View Product Details                    | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Displays a published product's images, applied price and availability per size. The Member selects a configured size and a branch that sells it and has it available, then passes the item to the shopping cart. Sizes stopped from sale remain visible but cannot be selected.                  |
|  12 | Manage Products                         | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff or Manager creates draft products with 1–25 fixed sizes (5–29) and system-generated SKUs, updates content and images, manages branch stock (adds selling branches, sets quantity per size at each branch, stops or resumes sale at a branch), stops or resumes the sale of a size, and soft-deletes a product. Staff changes stock only at assigned branches. |
|  13 | Import Products from Excel              | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff or Manager uploads an `.xlsx` template to create multiple draft products. The system validates the whole file and previews row-level errors; products are created only after confirmation, on an all-or-nothing basis, with quantities for the selected branches. Images can then be attached to the new products in one place. |
|  14 | Submit Product or Price Proposal        | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff or Manager submits a new product, or a new price for an existing size, for Manager approval. Each size has at most one pending price proposal at a time.                                                                                                        |
|  15 | Approve Product and Price               | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | The Manager accepts, adjusts, or rejects with a reason a pending product or price proposal, including the Manager's own. AI price suggestions are advisory only.                                                                                                      |
|  16 | Apply Approved Price                    | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff or Manager applies an approved price as a separate action. The new price affects only new views and checkouts, not orders or holds already accepted.                                                                                                            |
|  17 | Publish or Unpublish Product            | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Publishes an approved product that has at least one usable image and an applied price for every size, or hides it from the public catalogue while keeping it in product management.                                                                                   |
|  18 | Manage Product Categories               | Product Management                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Staff or Manager creates, renames, activates, or deactivates product categories with system-wide unique names, and reviews the affected products before deactivation.                                                                                                 |
|  19 | Browse Workshop Packages                | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Displays available workshop packages with pricing, durations, and overview details.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|  20 | View Workshop Package Details           | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Displays comprehensive package specifications, included materials, tools, and terms.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|  21 | Create Workshop Booking                 | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Selects a workshop package before a schedule/slot and supported ring-design path, enters participant data, and holds a temporary seat.                                                                                                                                |
|  22 | Pay Workshop Deposit                    | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Pays a 50% deposit of the selected package via VNPay. The booking is confirmed only after a valid payment confirmation.                                                                                                                                               |
|  23 | Look Up Booking Details                 | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Allows customers to look up a booking by reference code and view details and QR check-in ticket.                                                                                                                                                                      |
|  24 | Check In Participant                    | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Confirms attendee presence at the workshop via QR code scanning or manual code lookup.                                                                                                                                                                                |
|  25 | Manage Workshop Schedule                | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Creates, modifies, or cancels workshop calendar sessions, shifts, and branch allocations.                                                                                                                                                                             |
|  26 | Assign Staff to Workshop Slot           | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Assigns designated instructors or goldsmith staff members to facilitate specific workshop sessions.                                                                                                                                                                   |
|  27 | Configure Workshop Time Slot            | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Defines the standard daily operating timeframes for workshop sessions.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|  28 | Configure Workshop Slot Capacity        | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Sets the maximum seat capacity and participant limits for specific workshop slots.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|  29 | Configure Supported Materials by Branch | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Configures the types of jewelry materials and ring options available for selection at each branch location.                                                                                                                                                           |
|  30 | Configure Holiday and Off-Day Exception | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Sets calendar overrides, closures, and special operating rules for holidays and off-days.                                                                                                                                                                             |
|  31 | Synchronize Workshop Schedule           | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Exchanges enabled availability updates with Klook while Mland remains the capacity source of truth; Klook must obtain availability/hold before confirmation.                                                                                                          |
|  32 | Synchronize Workshop Booking            | Workshop Management                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Sends enabled Mland confirmed-booking updates to Klook and records Klook bookings confirmed through the Mland availability/hold flow; Klook cancellations are not consumed.                                                                                           |
|  33 | Chat with Basic AI Support              | AI Chatbox Analysis and Suggestion                                                                                                                                                                                          | Answers customer natural language inquiries regarding products, workshops, and store FAQs.                                                                                                                                                                            |
|  34 | Analyze Ring Design from Image          | AI Chatbox Analysis and Suggestion                                                                                                                                                                                          | Analyzes an authorized independent Design Request image, identifies candidate design components, and returns advisory evidence for human review.                                                                                                                      |
|  35 | Approve Custom Design Pricing           | AI Chatbox Analysis and Suggestion                                                                                                                                                                                          | Aggregates configured inputs to suggest a price for a new workshop package or retail product. The Manager, not AI, decides the published price; this does not price a custom-manufacturing order.                                                                     |
|  36 | Select Available Ring Model             | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Chooses a pre-designed, ready-to-make ring template available in the system catalog.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|  37 | Configure Workshop Ring                 | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Configures the ring to be made in a workshop by assembling compatible system-provided bands, gems, and engravings.                                                                                                                                                    |
|  38 | Chat with Staff Consultant              | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Initiates direct real-time consultation messaging with a jewelry consultant for custom design advice.                                                                                                                                                                 |
|  39 | Submit Booking Design Image             | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | A Member submits an image as a ring-design request within a workshop booking so that AI analysis and human review can be performed.                                                                                                                                   |
|  40 | Review Custom Design Request            | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Staff or Manager reviews an independent Design Request according to the approved Feature-004 authority policy and records the outcome.                                                                                                                                |
|  41 | View Custom Design Request Decision     | Personalized Design and Quote Workflow                                                                                                                                                                                      | Allows the authorized owner to view the safe recorded outcome of the reviewed independent Design Request.                                                                                                                                                             |
|  42 | View Loyalty Point Balance              | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays the Member's own point balance, held points, available points (balance minus held points), and current point value. Points do not expire and there are no membership tiers.                                                                                  |
|  43 | View Loyalty Point History              | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays a chronological ledger of earned, redeemed, released, and refunded points, each linked to its retail order or workshop booking.                                                                                                                              |
|  44 | Redeem Loyalty Points                   | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The Member chooses to use points as a non-cash discount on a retail order or workshop booking, from the minimum per use up to the Manager-configured cap, when the selected voucher allows points. Points are held on acceptance and deducted after verified payment, or when a valid 0 VND order or booking is accepted. |
|  45 | Manage Automatic Product Discounts      | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The Manager creates, edits, pauses, or resumes code-free automatic discounts for products. They always combine with points; when several apply, the largest discount is used.                                                                                         |
|  46 | Manage Voucher Code                     | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The Manager creates product or workshop vouchers with a system-generated code, scope (product vouchers may be limited to one or more branches), discount, cap, minimum spend, total and per-Member limits, and point-combination option, then edits allowed terms, pauses or resumes them, and tracks usage.                    |
|  47 | View and Save Vouchers                  | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | Guest or Member views public voucher cards and their conditions; a signed-in Member saves codes to a personal Voucher Wallet. Saving does not reserve a use or guarantee eligibility. Product pages show the largest automatic discount as a reduced price with the original price struck through, and vouchers as labels only. |
|  48 | Apply Voucher to Order or Booking       | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The Member applies one voucher or automatic discount to a retail order, based on eligible product lines (and, for a branch-limited voucher, lines at branches in scope) excluding shipping, or one workshop voucher to a booking before the deposit is calculated, discounting only the tickets in scope. Ineligible codes are rejected with a reason. A fee of 0 VND after discounts is accepted. |
|  49 | Earn Loyalty Points                     | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The system awards points once per transaction based on the amount actually paid: after verified full payment of a retail order, or when a workshop booking is completed and fully paid; for a multi-ticket booking, each completed ticket earns its share once. |
|  50 | Release or Refund Promotion Benefits    | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The system releases held points and voucher uses when an order, branch order or booking expires or a retail order is cancelled before payment, and refunds the original points and voucher use after a valid or Mland workshop cancellation, per ticket where applicable, exactly once. |
|  51 | Manage Loyalty Point Policy             | Loyalty and Promotion Management                                                                                                                                                                                            | The Manager configures the earning rate, point value, minimum points per use, and point-discount cap. Changes apply only to transactions accepted afterwards.                                                                                                         |
|  52 | Add Product to Cart                     | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Adds a selected retail product with chosen variant options (size, engraving) into the cart.                                                                                                                                                                           |
|  53 | Manage Shopping Cart                    | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays current cart contents, updates item quantities, and removes items from the cart.                                                                                                                                                                             |
|  54 | Checkout Retail Order                   | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Revalidates the whole cart, snapshots prices, applies an eligible voucher and/or points, and initiates a 15-minute full-cart payment hold; no partial order is created.                                                                                               |
|  55 | Pay for Retail Order                    | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Pays the full retail order through VNPay. A valid signed VNPay confirmation, rather than the browser Return URL, completes the sale and deducts held quantities.                                                                                                      |
|  56 | View Purchase Order History             | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays a Member's complete chronological list of past and ongoing retail orders.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|  57 | View Order Details                      | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Displays payment and internal fulfilment status for a retail order; it does not provide delivery tracking.                                                                                                                                                            |
|  58 | Review & Confirm Retail Order           | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Staff reviews a verified paid retail order, begins its internal preparation, and changes the order status to `Preparing`.                                                                                                                                             |
|  59 | Fulfill Retail Order                    | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | After verified payment, records the Member's choice of store pickup or GHTK fulfilment, coordinates preparation for that method, and updates the order status accordingly.                                                                                            |
|  60 | Record Customer Pickup                  | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Verifies customer identity and changes the status of a ready-for-pickup order to `Picked Up`.                                                                                                                                                                         |
|  61 | Mark Order as Prepared for Carrier      | Order Management and Fulfillment                                                                                                                                                                                            | Records that a prepared retail order was manually handed to GHTK and changes the order status to `Handed to GHTK`; it does not call GHTK APIs or track delivery.                                                                                                      |
|  62 | Configure System Operational Parameters | Administrative Parameter Configuration                                                                                                                                                                                      | Configures operational values, including custom-order minimum deadline, unavailable deadline dates, and maximum queue size.                                                                                                                                           |
|  63 | Manage Third-Party API Credentials      | Administrative Parameter Configuration                                                                                                                                                                                      | Maintains approved credentials and endpoints for VNPay, Klook, Gemini, Google Maps/Places, Mland SMTP, and cloud storage.                                                                                                                                             |
|  64 | View Dashboard                          | Administrative Parameter Configuration                                                                                                                                                                                      | Visualizes consolidated business analytics, workshop utilization, retail revenue, and branch KPIs.                                                                                                                                                                    |
|  65 | View Public Store Reviews and Ratings   | Public Store Information                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Displays store locations and permitted public Google rating/review information with required attribution and a link to Google Maps; Places content is not persisted.                                                                                                  |
|  66 | Submit Store Review                     | Public Store Information                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Opens the store's Google Maps review link. Mland does not capture internal feedback or submit a review to Google.                                                                                                                                                     |
|  67 | Submit Custom Order                     | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | A Member submits a custom-manufacturing order using a reference image or system-supported manual configuration, optional requested deadline, and fulfilment method. AI does not decide the order.                                                                     |
|  68 | Configure Custom Queue                  | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | A Manager or Admin configures the minimum deadline, dates on which a deadline cannot be selected, and the maximum custom-order queue size.                                                                                                                            |
|  69 | Pay Custom Deposit                      | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Pays a 50% deposit of the wax-package price through VNPay. An auto-accepted request holds one queue slot for up to 15 minutes while payment confirmation is pending; the slot is released without valid confirmation.                                                 |
|  70 | View Custom Order                       | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Allows the Member to view the custom-manufacturing order and its current internal status.                                                                                                                                                                             |
|  71 | Record Final Custom Balance             | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Staff or Manager records the final balance after the custom item is completed. The balance consists of the remaining 50% of the custom-order price plus any approved additional charges incurred during customization.                                                |
|  72 | Pay Custom Balance                      | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Sends the Member an email request and accepts VNPay payment for the recorded final balance before fulfilment.                                                                                                                                                         |
|  73 | Fulfil Custom Order                     | Custom Manufacturing                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Records Member pickup or Staff's manual handoff of the completed paid item to GHTK.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
