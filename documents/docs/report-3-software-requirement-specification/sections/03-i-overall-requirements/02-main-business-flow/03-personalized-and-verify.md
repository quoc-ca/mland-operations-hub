### 2.3 Personalized Design & Verification

_Trigger_:

- A Member starts the personalized design process by entering finger parameters and selecting materials and a ring-design method, such as choosing an available design, configuring a design manually, or uploading an image for AI analysis.


_End condition_:

- The process ends when the design is saved and approved for booking, or when the request is rejected with a recorded reason. Requests requiring human verification remain pending until Staff or Manager records a decision.
