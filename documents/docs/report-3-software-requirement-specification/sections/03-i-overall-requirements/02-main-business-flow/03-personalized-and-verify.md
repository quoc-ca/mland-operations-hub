### 2.3 Personalized Design & Verification

_Trigger_:

- A Member starts the personalized design process by entering finger parameters and selecting materials and a ring-design method, such as choosing an available design, configuring a design manually, or submitting an independent custom design request with a description or reference images for human review.


_End condition_:

- The process ends when the design is saved with an advisory workshop recommendation, or when an independent request is accepted, rejected, or returned for clarification with a recorded explanation. A request requiring human review remains pending until Staff or Manager records a decision.
