### 3.1 Workshop Management

#### 3.1.4 Pay Workshop Deposit

<table>
<tr><td>Primary Actors</td><td>Guest, Member</td><td>Secondary Actors</td><td>Payment Gateway, Mail Gateway</td></tr>
<tr><td>Description</td><td colspan="3">As a Guest or Member, I want to pay the required workshop deposit through VNPay so that my pending workshop booking can be confirmed after the system verifies a valid payment notification.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Preconditions</td><td colspan="3">1. The platform is available.<br>2. A pending workshop booking and its package invoice have been created by UC17 Create Workshop Booking.<br>3. The booking has a valid temporary capacity hold.<br>4. The required deposit amount is available and equals 50% of the selected package price.<br>5. VNPay credentials and the payment endpoint are configured.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Postconditions</td><td colspan="3">• If valid payment confirmation is received, exactly one deposit transaction is recorded, the invoice deposit is marked paid, and the booking is confirmed.<br>• A booking reference code and QR check-in ticket are generated for a confirmed booking.<br>• A confirmation notification is sent to the booking contact email.<br>• If payment is pending, failed, expired, invalid, or duplicated, the booking remains unconfirmed and the capacity hold is released when its hold window expires.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Normal<br>Sequence/Flow</td><td colspan="3"><em>Pay Workshop Deposit</em><br>1. The actor reviews the pending booking and deposit amount.<br>2. The actor starts payment for the workshop deposit.<br>3. The system creates a VNPay payment request for the required 50% deposit and redirects the actor to VNPay.<br>4. The actor completes or cancels the payment on VNPay.<br>5. VNPay returns the payment result through the approved signed confirmation mechanism.<br>6. The system validates the signature, transaction amount, booking reference, payment status, and duplicate-event status while the capacity hold remains active.<br>7. The system records one verified deposit transaction.<br>8. The system marks the invoice deposit as paid and changes the booking status to confirmed.<br>9. The system generates the booking code and QR check-in ticket.<br>10. The system sends a booking confirmation notification to the actor's contact email.<br>11. The system displays the confirmed booking and payment result. If the actor is a Member, the booking, invoice, and transaction are saved in Member history. If the actor is a Guest, the system may offer optional Member registration.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Alternative<br>Sequences/Flows</td><td colspan="3"><em>Step 3 — Payment request cannot be created</em><br>The system displays an error and keeps the booking unconfirmed. The actor may retry while the capacity hold remains active.<br><br><em>Step 4 — Actor cancels or abandons payment</em><br>The system keeps the booking in payment-pending/unconfirmed status. The capacity hold remains only until its expiry time.<br><br><em>Step 6 — VNPay confirmation is invalid or the amount does not match</em><br>The system rejects the confirmation, records the failed/invalid result for audit purposes, and does not confirm the booking.<br><br><em>Step 6 — Browser Return URL is received without valid server-side confirmation</em><br>The system does not treat the redirect as payment confirmation. The booking remains unconfirmed until a valid signed IPN or approved signed recovery result is received.<br><br><em>Step 6 — Duplicate payment event</em><br>The system ignores the duplicate event and does not create a second transaction or alter the already recorded result.<br><br><em>Step 6 — Payment is pending or failed</em><br>The system displays the pending/failed status and keeps the booking unconfirmed. The actor may retry if the hold is still active.<br><br><em>After Step 6 — Hold or payment link has expired</em><br>The system rejects late confirmation and releases the temporary capacity hold. The actor must create a new booking if they want to try again.<br><br><em>Step 10 — Confirmation notification cannot be sent</em><br>The booking remains confirmed and the system records the notification failure for retry or operational follow-up.</td></tr>
<tr><td>Business Rule</td><td colspan="3">BR-18-01, BR-18-02, BR-18-03, BR-18-04</td></tr>
</table>

<table>
<tr style="background-color:#f4cccc"><th>ID</th><th>Rule Definition</th></tr>
<tr><td>BR-18-01</td><td>The workshop deposit is 50% of the selected package price and must be paid through the configured VNPay payment flow.</td></tr>
<tr><td>BR-18-02</td><td>A workshop booking is confirmed only after one valid, signed, non-duplicate VNPay confirmation with the expected booking reference and amount is received.</td></tr>
<tr><td>BR-18-03</td><td>A VNPay browser Return URL alone never confirms payment; the system requires the approved signed IPN or signed QueryDR recovery result.</td></tr>
<tr><td>BR-18-04</td><td>The VNPay payment link expires after 10 minutes. The related workshop capacity hold lasts at most 15 minutes and is released without valid confirmation.</td></tr>
</table>
