## 4. Design Request and Human Review (Feature-004)

> **Current baseline (2026-11-20):** This section replaces the former AI Chatbox Analysis and Suggestion scope. Feature-004 has no AI provider or automated image/design evaluation. Guest and Member submit independent requests with up to five images; Staff or Manager accepts/rejects with a mandatory explanation. Legacy child files remain historical until the report is structurally migrated.

> **2026-11-22 terminology and reference update:** The customer flow is named
> **Yêu cầu chế tác**. It may include an owner-visible Feature-012 design in
> any readable state; the request stores an immutable snapshot. The saved-design
> page can open a Feature-013 consultation for Guest or Member.
