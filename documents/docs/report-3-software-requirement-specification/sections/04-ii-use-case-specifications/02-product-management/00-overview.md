## 2. Product Management

This section covers browsing, creating, updating and publishing retail products.

**Shared product requirements**

- Product name is 1–200 characters and description 1–5,000 after trimming. Proposed/applied prices are positive whole VND; decimals and currency conversion are not supported.
- Mland supplies predefined size/engraving options, including “Không khắc” (No engraving); the initial option list remains required. Variants have stable identities and independent prices/quantities; no editable parent stock total or separate variant-publication state is added.
- Each product supports up to 10 shared images, 5 MB each, in JPEG/PNG/WebP, with 1–200-character alternative text and distinct display positions. A usable image is successfully stored, readable and in an allowed format. Draft media requires product authority; public media follows product eligibility.
- Keep referenced/history media. Delete eligible unreferenced files after 30 days and failed unlinked uploads after 24 hours; removing a reference alone never authorizes file deletion. Storage-provider selection belongs to planning.
- Preserve legacy orders/holds without guessed variant options; old product-only records require reviewed variant configuration before new checkout. Existing valid holds retain frozen terms within original deadlines.
- Catalogue attempts retain normally append-only actor, target, action, time, actual success/rejection/failure, reason and permitted context/references for five years. Rejected/failed attempts are never recorded as success; credentials, tokens and avoidable personal data are excluded.
