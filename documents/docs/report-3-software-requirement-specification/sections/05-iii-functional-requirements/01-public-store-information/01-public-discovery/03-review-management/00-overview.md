# Review Management

Date created: 2026-11-03  
Date updated: 2026-11-03  
Author: Ngô Tiến Đạt  
Last updated by: Ngô Tiến Đạt

Feature-011 lets a Guest or Member review a workshop session after a
`CHECKED_IN` attendance or a retail product after the order line is
`PICKED_UP`/`DELIVERED`. A valid review is published as `PUBLIC` immediately.
Public listing and aggregates exclude `HIDDEN` and `REMOVED`, and public
identity is partially masked.

The feature does not review branches, import/write Google Places reviews, use
AI moderation, accept media, send notifications or create threaded replies.
Staff may hide any review; Manager may hide/remove and maintain one current
reply; Admin has technical/audit access only.
