#### 1.1.1 Home Page

![](assets/screens/live/01-home-page.png)

This screen allows visitors and signed-in users to:

- Read Mland's introduction and open the workshop list or the product catalogue from the hero buttons.
- Browse up to three published workshop packages, with the price after the best automatic discount, and the first page of published products as product cards.
- Open the service entry points: workshop booking, ready rings, custom crafting, AI advisor, booking lookup and order tracking.
- As a Member, see a quick bar with a greeting, the next confirmed workshop session and the number of retail orders.
- As Staff, a Manager or an Admin, open the internal portals of their role.
- Use the shared top bar to open workshops, products, vouchers, booking lookup, reviews and the cart, to sign in, or to open the account menu of their role.

The page is public. When no workshop package or product can be loaded, built-in sample packages and rings are shown instead. Only visitors and Members see the custom crafting, AI advisor and order tracking entries.

**Field Description**

| Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Session notice | Shown when the user was sent here with a session message, for example after a sign-out; can be dismissed with ×. |
| Member quick bar | Members only. Shows "Hello, {name}!" and the start time of the next confirmed workshop session, or "You have no upcoming workshops." **🎟️ View ticket & QR code** (only when a session exists) opens Booking Lookup with the booking code. **📦 Orders (n)** opens My Retail Orders. |
| Hero | **🔨 BOOK A WORKSHOP NOW** opens Workshop Package List; **💍 EXPLORE READY RINGS** opens Product Catalogue. |
| Brand story | Read-only text about the studio and three commitments. |
| Featured crafting workshop packages | Up to three published packages with image, name, description, number of sessions, participant range and price. A discounted price and offer badges appear when an automatic discount or voucher applies. **Details & Booking** or **Reserve your spot now** opens the package details. |
| Ready rings | The first page of published products, newest first, as a sideways strip of product cards with ‹ › buttons and **See all rings →** (opens Product Catalogue). Each card shows the discounted price, offer badges and the add-to-cart button. The sample rings shown on failure have **Details →**, which opens Product Catalogue. |
| Service ecosystem | Entry cards: **🔨 Book a workshop**, **💍 Buy Ready Rings**, **🎨 Custom crafting**, **🤖 AI advisor**, **🎫 Ticket & booking lookup** and **🚚 Order tracking**. For a Member, the order card shows the order count when it is above zero. |
| Internal portals | Staff, Manager and Admin only. Staff and Manager see **Staff Operations**, **Design Review** and **Product Management**; a Manager also sees **Promotion Management**; an Admin sees **Admin Accounts**. The link of the viewer's own role is highlighted. Each target route still checks the role. |
| Customer reviews | Three fixed sample testimonials with a fixed "4.9 / 5.0" rating; they are not read from Public Review List. |
| Footer | Fixed text: the Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City showroom addresses, the hotline, opening hours (09:00 - 21:00 daily), a policy line (terms of service, privacy policy, warranty commitment; not links) and the copyright line. |
| Mobile navigation | On small screens, a bottom bar to **Home**, **Workshop**, **Rings**, **Lookup** and **Sign in** or **Account** (password page for the Carrier). |
