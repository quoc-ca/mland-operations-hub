#### 8.4.1 Carrier Portal

![](assets/screens/live/96-carrier-portal.png)

This screen allows the Carrier (GHTK) to:

- See the partner identity: display name, sign-in email, role and nationwide branch scope.
- See today's GHTK work: new shipments today, shipments waiting for pickup and shipments on the way.
- Open Carrier Orders to record the courier's status steps.
- Open the password page to change the password.

This is the Carrier's home: the Carrier is always sent here after sign-in, and only the CARRIER role can open it. Another role, or a suspended or not yet set-up account, is sent to the not-found page.

**Field Description**

| Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Title and badge | "Carrier partner portal (GHTK)" with the badge "CARRIER · {status}" and "The portal for the GHTK delivery partner: manage pickups, deliveries and shipment progress." |
| Load error | "Could not load the partner account." |
| Partner identity | "Partner / representative:" (the part of the sign-in email before "@"), "Sign-in email:", "System role:" and "Branch scope:" "Nationwide (not limited to one branch)". |
| 🚚 Shipments to deliver | "Pick up packed retail jewellery orders and deliver them to customers with GHTK.", then "{n} new shipments today" (created today, Vietnam time) and "{waiting} waiting for pickup · {delivering} on the way". The counts are hidden when the shipments cannot be read. |
| **View shipments →** | Opens Carrier Orders. |
| 🔑 Security & password | "Change your password regularly to keep the carrier account safe." with **Change password →**, which opens the password page. |
