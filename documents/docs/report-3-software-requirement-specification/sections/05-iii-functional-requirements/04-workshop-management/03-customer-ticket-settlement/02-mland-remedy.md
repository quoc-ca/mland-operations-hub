#### 4.3.2 Mland Remedy

This screen allows the buyer of a workshop ticket (Member or Guest), or an in-scope Staff member or Manager answering for the buyer, to:

- See that Mland cancelled a session of their ticket and the deadline to answer.
- Choose a 100% refund of the ticket to their bank account, or one of the offered equivalent sessions.
- Narrow the offered sessions by location and date.
- As a Guest, Staff member or Manager, verify the buyer with a 6-digit code sent to the booking's stored email.
- Confirm the answer in a dialog; the answer cannot be changed afterwards.
- See the final outcome (moved or being refunded) once the ticket has been answered.

A Member is recognised by the sign-in. Staff can act only on assigned sessions. Offers also apply to tickets whose bill is already finalized. Without an answer before the deadline, the ticket is refunded. The offer carries no payment; the refund goes to the bank account the buyer enters later through the emailed Refund Account link. A Member's refunded ticket also gets its used points back, and the voucher is restored if still valid.

**Field Description**

| Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Title and intro | **Your session was cancelled** with **We had to cancel a session of your ticket. Choose a replacement session or a refund.** |
| Ticket and deadline | **Ticket {code}** and **Answer before {date time}. Without an answer the ticket is refunded.** while the answer is pending. |
| Outcome | After an answer: **Your ticket was moved to the replacement session.** or **Your ticket is being refunded.** |
| Voucher notice | Shown only when a voucher of the ticket expired and was not restored; the refund is not affected. |
| Choose one | Group of radio options, with no option chosen at first. |
| Refund option | **Refund this ticket (100%) to my bank account**, the first option. |
| Location / Date | Filters for the offered sessions. **Location** appears only when sessions are at more than one location; options: **All locations** and each location. Default: All locations, no date. A filter change clears a chosen session. |
| Session options | One radio per offered session that can still be chosen, with location and start–end time. **No matching replacement session.** when none matches. |
| Buyer verification | Guest, Staff or Manager. **Contact email** or **Contact phone** (exactly one) and **Send code** send a code to the booking's stored email. **Code (6 digits)** and **Verify** complete the proof; **Send again** resends. |
| Send my answer | Enabled when a choice is made and the actor is the signed-in Member or the buyer is verified. Opens **Confirm your answer**, which restates the choice and that it cannot be changed. |
| Refusal messages | **That session has no seat left or is no longer sold at that location. Choose another session or ask for a refund.**, **That session is not one of the offered sessions.** or **The time to answer has passed. The ticket is refunded.** |
| Not found | **We could not find an offer for this ticket.** |
