# III. Functional Requirements

- The system shall expose three operating sessions: 09:30–12:00, 13:00–15:30, and 16:00–18:30. A location/session is bookable only when its capacity is configured.
- The system shall allow Guest and Member bookings and create an invoice with a 50% deposit due.
- The system shall confirm a booking only from valid payment confirmation, not from a browser redirect.
- The system shall permit a Member, but not a Guest, to complete ready-ring retail checkout.
- The system shall preserve invoice adjustment audit information and customer consent.
- The system shall record custody evidence for continuation work and a minimal carrier-handoff record for retail delivery.

Exact catalogue components, price rules, capacity values, gateway provider contract, cancellation/refund rules, image-retention wording, and delivery-data retention are `TBD`.
