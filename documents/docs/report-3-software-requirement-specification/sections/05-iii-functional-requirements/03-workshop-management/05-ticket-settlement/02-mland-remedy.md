#### 3.5.2 Mland Remedy

This screen allows the buyer of a workshop ticket (Member or Guest) to:

- See that Mland cancelled a session of their ticket and the deadline to answer.
- Choose one of the offered equivalent sessions, or a 100% refund of the ticket to their bank account.
- As a Guest, verify as the buyer with a 6-digit code sent to the booking's stored email before answering; a Member is recognised by the sign-in.
- Confirm the answer in a dialog; the answer cannot be changed afterwards.
- See the final outcome (moved or being refunded) once the ticket has been answered.

The buyer answers. The server also accepts an answer from an in-scope Staff member or a Manager with the buyer's code proof, but this screen offers the code step only to signed-out visitors, so it cannot send such a counter answer. Offers also apply to tickets whose folio is already finalized. Without an answer before the deadline the ticket is refunded. The offer carries no payment; a refund is paid to the bank account the buyer enters later through the emailed Refund Account link.

| Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Title and intro | "Ca của bạn đã bị hủy" with the instruction to choose a replacement session or a refund. |
| Ticket and deadline | "Vé {code}" and "Hãy trả lời trước {date time}. Nếu không trả lời, vé sẽ được hoàn tiền." while the answer is pending. |
| Outcome | After an answer: "Vé của bạn đã được chuyển sang ca thay thế." or "Vé của bạn đang được hoàn tiền." |
| "Chọn một" options | One radio per offered session with location and start–end time; a session that can no longer be chosen is disabled and marked "(không khả dụng)". |
| Refund option | "Hoàn tiền vé này (100%) vào tài khoản ngân hàng của tôi". |
| "Xác minh người mua" | Guest only. One contact (email or phone) sends a code to the booking's stored email; "Mã (6 chữ số)" and "Xác minh" complete the proof, "Gửi lại" resends. |
| "Gửi câu trả lời" | Enabled when a choice is made and the buyer is a Member or has been verified; opens the confirmation dialog "Xác nhận câu trả lời của bạn". |
| Refusal messages | The chosen session is full or no longer sold at that location; the session is not one of the offered ones; or the answer deadline has passed and the ticket is refunded. |
| Voucher notice | Shown only when a voucher of the ticket expired and was not restored; the refund is not affected. |
