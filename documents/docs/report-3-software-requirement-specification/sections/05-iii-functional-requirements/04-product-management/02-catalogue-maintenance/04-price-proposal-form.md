#### 4.2.4 Price Proposal Form

![](assets/screens/50-price-proposal-form.png)

The price proposal form is the **Đề xuất giá** section of Product Management Details. It allows Staff and Managers to:

- Review each size's applied price and its full price history.
- Enter a new proposed price for one size, or a common or individual price for several sizes at once.
- Save proposals as drafts, then submit them to the Manager for review.
- Apply a Manager-approved price as a separate action.

Saving or submitting a proposal never changes the applied price or the publication state.

| Field Name | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Size heading | "Size N — Giá đang áp dụng" shows the price customers currently pay for that size. |
| Price history (Lịch sử giá) | Per proposal: round (Lần), state (Nháp, Chờ duyệt, Đã duyệt or Bị từ chối), proposed amount, approved amount, whether it was applied and the rejection reason. |
| Submitter / decider (Người gửi / Người quyết định) | Who submitted and decided each proposal, the submitter's branch and the time. |
| New proposed price (Đề xuất giá mới) | Positive whole-VND amount; **Lưu** saves it as a draft and **Hủy** discards it. |
| Gửi duyệt giá | Submits the saved draft to the Manager. |
| Sửa giá đề xuất nhiều size | Bulk editor showing the applied price, current proposal, new proposal and difference per size, with "Giá đề xuất chung (₫)", **Lưu và gửi duyệt** and **Chỉ lưu nháp**. |
| Áp dụng giá đã duyệt | Applies an approved, not yet applied price to the size. |
