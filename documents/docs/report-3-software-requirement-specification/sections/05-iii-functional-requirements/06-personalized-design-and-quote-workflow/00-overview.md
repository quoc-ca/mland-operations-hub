## 6. Personalized Design and Quote Workflow

This section covers the two ways a Member can personalize a ring. In the workshop ring designer, a Member configures one ring for a selected workshop package, saves it and receives an advisory evaluation and workshop recommendation. A Guest may browse the entry point and try a local preview but must sign in before saving or evaluating. Through design requests, a Member describes a ring with text, reference images or a saved ring design, and Staff or Managers review each request and write the decision and explanation themselves. The system makes no AI or automatic business decision: prices, difficulty and recommendations shown to customers are advisory, and only a human reviewer accepts or rejects a request.
