## 6. Personalized Design and Quote Workflow

This section covers the two ways a customer can personalize a ring. In the independent workshop ring designer, a Member configures a ring from catalogue-controlled options (a Guest may only preview locally), saves it and receives an advisory evaluation and workshop recommendation. The Member may either explore the recommended workshop, open a consultation, or use the saved design as an optional immutable reference for a custom-manufacturing request. Through design requests, a Member describes a ring with text, reference images or a saved ring design, and Staff or Managers review each request and write the decision and explanation themselves. An accepted request continues through quote preparation, Manager approval, one full payment before quote expiry, production and fulfilment. The system makes no AI or automatic business decision: prices, difficulty and recommendations shown to customers are advisory, and only authorized human staff perform review, quote approval and order fulfilment.
