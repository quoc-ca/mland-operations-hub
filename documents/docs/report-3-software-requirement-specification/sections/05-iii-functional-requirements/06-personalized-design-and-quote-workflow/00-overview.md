## 6. Personalized Design and Quote Workflow

This section covers the two ways a customer can personalize a ring. In the independent workshop ring designer, a Member configures a ring from catalogue-controlled options (a Guest may only preview locally), saves it and receives an advisory evaluation and workshop recommendation. Through design requests, a Member describes a ring with text, reference images or a saved ring design, and Staff or Managers review each request and write the decision and explanation themselves. The system makes no AI or automatic business decision: prices, difficulty and recommendations shown to customers are advisory, and only a human reviewer accepts or rejects a request.
