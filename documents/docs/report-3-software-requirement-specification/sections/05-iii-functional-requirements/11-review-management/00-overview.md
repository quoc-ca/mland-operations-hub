## 11. Review Management

Review Management stores Mland reviews for workshop sessions and retail
products. Guests can read public reviews and aggregates, but all eligibility,
creation and editing actions require an authenticated Member account. A Member
can review only an eligible checked-in workshop attendance or picked-up or
delivered retail order line, and each source can have at most one review.

Reviews are public immediately after validation. Staff may hide a review;
Managers may hide, remove, restore and reply according to the moderation
workflow. Public responses expose only safe review content and masked identity.
The system does not review branches, store Google Maps/Places reviews or write
back to an external review provider.
