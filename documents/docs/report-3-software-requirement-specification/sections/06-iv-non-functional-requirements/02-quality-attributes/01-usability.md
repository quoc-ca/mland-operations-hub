### 2.1 Usability

| Requirement | V1 requirement | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer journey clarity | The UI must present the ring journey in business order: account/access, catalogue or workshop selection, design/customisation, feasibility or estimate, payment, fulfilment, and status. Each step must show the current status and the next permitted action. | Scenario walkthrough and acceptance test for workshop, ready-ring, and custom-design journeys. |
| Booking and payment feedback | The UI must clearly distinguish an available item/slot, a temporary hold, a confirmed booking/order, an expired hold, and an unverified payment. A browser Return URL must never be presented as payment confirmation. | Functional test with valid, duplicate, invalid, and late payment notifications. |
| Validation and recovery | Forms must identify the invalid field or rejected business condition in plain language, preserve valid entered values where safe, and offer a next action such as retry, choose another slot, contact the shop, or continue outside the unavailable provider. | Negative-path usability test. |
| Role-appropriate operation | Staff, Manager, Owner, Admin, and Admin Technical views must expose only the actions permitted by the role and must use domain terminology consistently. | Role-based walkthrough against the permission matrix. |
| Operational visibility | Staff and Managers must be able to distinguish pending, confirmed, expired, cancelled/withdrawn where applicable, ready-for-pickup, and handed-off records without relying on provider screens. | Workflow acceptance test and record-state review. |
| Accessibility and language baseline | Customer-facing screens must support the approved bilingual journey and keyboard-operable forms. The final accessibility conformance level, supported browser matrix, and language glossary are project baselines to be approved before UAT. | Accessibility review and browser/UAT checklist once the baseline is approved. |
| Training | The Reports do not approve a numeric training-time target. Training material must cover account access, booking/payment states, design review, fulfilment handoff, and the role-specific approval boundaries before UAT. | Training checklist and supervised task completion. |
