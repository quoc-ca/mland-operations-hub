### 2.1 Usability

These requirements support Report 1's bilingual journey and shared operational context. Criteria make use-case interactions observable without claiming measured training time or accessibility certification.

| ID | Requirement | Verification criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NFR-USA-01 | Customer navigation, instructions, validation, payment/booking/order status labels and recovery guidance shall consistently support Vietnamese and English. | Walk through browsing, booking, Member checkout, lookup and errors in each language. Application-controlled labels preserve meaning, amounts and state. User text, catalogue translations and provider pages are not assumed automatically translated. Basis: Report 1 Purpose/Background. |
| NFR-USA-02 | Product/package selection shall expose needed choice information without presenting indicative values as frozen purchase terms. | UC11 shows variants, selected-variant price/currency and logical availability; UC16 shows package price, duration, deposit and terms. Cart is indicative; checkout presents the complete quote for acceptance. |
| NFR-USA-03 | Booking/payment screens shall distinguish pending, verified success, failure and expiry and show applicable link/hold deadlines. | Creation or a successful-looking VNPay return cannot display confirmed payment without accepted evidence. Identify amount, recorded state and next action; reopening/retrying implies no new deadline. See UC17/UC18 and UC45/UC46. |
| NFR-USA-04 | Invalid input shall receive correction guidance; empty, unavailable and retrieval-failure states shall be distinguishable. | Test filters, quantities, media and recipient fields. Identify the affected criterion safely. Empty catalogue/cart/history differs from errors; retain safe editable input where correction is allowed. See UC11–UC14 and UC43–UC51. |
| NFR-USA-05 | Quote changes, invalid-line removal and stale conflicts shall require explicit review before fresh submission, without silent variant substitution, benefit removal or payable changes. | Change price/quantity/benefits during checkout. Explain changes and offer review/reselection without accepting a partial order. See UC43–UC46. |
| NFR-USA-06 | Distinguish booking analysis, catalogue price advice and custom manufacturing; identify AI output as advisory. | UC29–UC31 show context/review status without implying final AI price/manufacturing decisions. Custom orders are not AI-approved booking designs. See Report 2 Scope and Report 3 Context. |
| NFR-USA-07 | Distinguish preparation, pickup readiness and actual handoff; expose only currently permitted actions. | Member retail method choice occurs after verified payment and before preparation. `prepared_for_carrier` is not handoff/courier delivery. Viewing/verifying alone does not complete pickup. See UC48–UC53. |
| NFR-USA-08 | Operational views shall preserve reference, branch, participant/variant context and relevant history for authorized review before confirmation. | Staff checks matching booking/owner/order and current state before check-in/handover. History preserves terms, identifies unknown legacy values and hides retired recipient data. See UC20 and UC47–UC53. |
| NFR-USA-09 | Public store information shall show required Google attribution/link; review action shall identify/open the external Google destination. | Inspect presentation and follow the link. Missing provider content is unavailable, not an internal review/rating. See Report 2 C-05 and GBR-12. |

Device/browser coverage, accessibility conformance and measured task/training targets remain NFR-OPEN-03. Agree and test them before claiming compatibility/accessibility across particular environments.
