## 2. Quality Attributes

These cross-cutting criteria apply alongside usability, performance and security. They express reliability, consistency and recovery properties required by Report 3 workflows without adding an availability percentage, backup product or production topology.

| ID | Requirement | Verification criterion and basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NFR-REL-01 | Failed/rejected commands shall not expose partially accepted results. Checkout shall accept the complete order, exact-variant/benefit holds and first attempt together or none. | Inject validation/save failures into UC12/UC13, UC17 and UC45. Preserve valid state; no partial catalogue group, confirmed booking, order or reservation bundle is reported successful. |
| NFR-REL-02 | Repeated/concurrent evidence or commands shall create at most one effective result where idempotency is defined. Identical checkout retry returns original order/attempt/deadlines; changed-input reuse rejects. | Replay IPN/QueryDR, Klook bookings, checkout, check-in and handoff/pickup. No duplicate sale, stock/points/quota deduction, booking, attendance or terminal handover. See UC18/UC20/UC28, UC45/UC46 and UC49–UC53; product creation gains no undocumented retry-deduplication guarantee. |
| NFR-REL-03 | Payment acceptance and unpaid-hold release shall be mutually exclusive. Late evidence shall not revive expired/released resources. | Race verification with expiry. Retail verification/acceptance must finish strictly before original expiry; first acceptance at/after expiry is an operational exception even if receipt/provider time was earlier. Duplicates never undo an accepted sale. See UC46. |
| NFR-REL-04 | Concurrent operations shall preserve logical capacity, exact-variant quantity, approved benefit limits and accepted snapshots. Stale conflicting edits shall require reload/review. | Compete for last seat/unit/benefit and submit conflicting edits: no over-allocation, substitution or silent overwrite. Variant quantity cannot fall below active holds. Group bookings count participants, not rows. See UC13/UC24 and UC43–UC51. |
| NFR-REL-05 | Provider/email failure shall preserve committed results and expose safe pending/failure states. Side-effect retries shall not repeat business transactions. | Simulate SMTP/Klook/AI/VNPay timeouts. Optional mail failure does not undo sale/readiness/handoff; mandatory verification stays incomplete; Klook failure preserves Mland capacity; AI failure grants no approval. Retry follows the [Background Job Inventory](../../03-i-overall-requirements/05-system-fuctionalities/03-background-job-inventory.md). |
| NFR-REL-06 | Frozen purchase/branch/approval/handoff facts shall survive configuration changes. Fulfilment shall follow the selected method's forward transitions only. | Change price/publication/branch activation after checkout. Valid holds retain terms/deadlines; history is not recalculated. Retail follows `paid → preparing → ready_for_pickup → picked_up` or `paid → preparing → prepared_for_carrier → handed_to_carrier`, with no backward/cross-terminal override. See UC13/UC14 and UC45–UC53. |
| NFR-REL-07 | Cleanup/recovery shall be repeatable without duplicate effects, extended deadlines, handoff-clock resets or restoring retired personal data. | Repeat expiry/media/anonymization jobs: release once, preserve disposal and minimized evidence. Restore must reapply retirement/expiry before exposing data; recovery/backup targets remain NFR-OPEN-02. |

Maintainability is supported by validated operational parameters, separate integration configuration and stable historical identities. UC54/UC55 preserve prior configuration on invalid changes and safely audit authorized changes. Architecture/dependency enforcement belongs to the software design specification; no extra architecture or maintainability metric is inferred.

Acceptance evidence shall identify tested NFR IDs, configuration, inputs/state, expected and observed results. Use synthetic accounts, orders, images and provider evidence. Load/restore/accessibility results shall state tested environments and limits rather than imply an unapproved production guarantee.
