## 3. Other Requirements

No additional standalone requirement is baselined under this heading in this edition. The approved requirements are recorded in Sections I–IV and in Appendix V.1–V.2. Any future item added here must have an approved source, a clear verification criterion, and traceability to the affected use case or requirement.

The legacy traceability matrix, data dictionary, prior requirement trackers, and historical open-issue material are not reproduced here as normative requirements. They remain archive source material until reviewed, reconciled with this report, and approved. Unresolved values remain tracked as `TBD` or in the NFR baseline register; they are not implied requirements.
