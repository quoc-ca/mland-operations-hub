## 1. Scope and Purpose

Mland V1 is an operations platform for a ring atelier. It connects customer discovery, workshop booking, personalised ring work, retail orders, and shop-side fulfilment without becoming a warehouse-management, ERP, accounting, or end-to-end logistics system. Report 3 remains the detailed source for use cases and rules; this section defines the V1 scope and operating boundaries. The rationale and traceability for the decisions below are in [07. Evidences](07-evidences.md).

V1 supports account and role-based access control, public catalogue, workshop packages and schedules, ring-design consultation, Member retail, loyalty points and vouchers, notifications, operational administration, an executive revenue dashboard, Google Maps information, and workshop synchronization with Klook. Staff operates catalogue products, images, prices, publication, and manually maintained available-to-sell quantities. Managers operate workshop schedules, capacity, material availability, holiday exceptions, promotions, vouchers, dashboards, and business approvals. Admins manage accounts, roles, access control, operating parameters, and approved integration credentials.

Guests and Members can browse published products, workshop packages, schedules, available slots, ring models, and public review information. They can book a workshop after selecting a package and supported design path, and may use basic AI support. A workshop booking is confirmed only after a successful VNPay payment for a deposit equal to 50% of the selected package price. Members may additionally maintain profiles, use retail checkout, use loyalty points or vouchers, and chat with Staff. Gemini image classification is an independent reference feature; it does not assess, price, accept, or reject a custom-manufacturing request.

All VNPay payment links expire 10 minutes after initiation. The associated workshop slot, custom-order queue slot, or entire retail cart is held for 15 minutes. A signed VNPay IPN is the normal confirmation channel; Mland verifies the signature, transaction reference, amount, and successful transaction status before applying the state transition, and safely ignores duplicate events. If no valid IPN has been recorded while the resource is still held, Mland may use signed VNPay QueryDR as a recovery confirmation. A browser return URL never confirms payment. If no successful evidence exists at minute 15, Mland releases the hold; any later evidence is a payment exception for manual shop follow-up and never automatically triggers fulfilment. This is an operational recovery control, not an accounting or reconciliation workflow. See [payment evidence](07-evidences.md#e-02--vnpay-payment-and-ipn) and [QueryDR evidence](07-evidences.md#e-03--vnpay-querydr).

Member retail supports cart management, whole-cart revalidation, price snapshots, and a 15-minute whole-cart hold. Invalid or unavailable cart lines are removed and reported; checkout creates no partial order. A verified VNPay full payment completes the sale and deducts the held quantities. After payment, the Member selects store pickup or fulfilment by Giao Hàng Tiết Kiệm (GHTK). GHTK fulfilment requires recipient and delivery-address data for that order. Staff records pickup or prepares and manually hands the order to GHTK. GHTK fees are outside the order and the shop's recorded responsibility ends at handoff; V1 does not integrate with GHTK APIs, quote fees, or track delivery. See [E-01 and E-10](07-evidences.md#e-01--mland-business-decision-record).

V1 supports a full custom-manufacturing flow for Members. A Member submits a reference image or a system-supported manual design/configuration, chooses a fulfilment method, and may provide a requested deadline. Managers and Admins configure the minimum deadline, dates on which deadlines cannot be selected, and the maximum custom-order queue size. If the Member supplies no deadline, the system assigns the earliest valid date. A request is automatically accepted only when its deadline is valid and capacity remains. An accepted request reserves one queue slot on deposit-payment initiation; the slot follows the same 10-minute payment-link and 15-minute hold policy, and remains reserved after successful deposit until pickup or carrier handoff.

The custom-manufacturing deposit is 50% of the wax-package price. When the item is ready, Staff or Manager records the final amount: the remaining 50% of the wax-package price plus actual surcharges from increased material weight or externally sourced accessories. The system emails the Member a request for the outstanding VNPay payment without requiring a line-item surcharge breakdown. A verified final VNPay payment is required before fulfilment is completed.

Google Maps is read-only. Mland embeds the shop map through Google Maps Embed and uses Google Places data to display permitted public rating/review information with the required attribution and links back to Google Maps. Mland does not persist Places review or rating content, collect an internal review, or submit a review to Google. See [Google Maps evidence](07-evidences.md#e-04--google-maps-embed-and-places-policy).

Klook is the V1 external workshop-registration partner. Mland remains the source of truth for workshop capacity: Klook must obtain Mland availability/hold before confirming a Klook booking, and Mland sends its confirmed bookings or availability updates to Klook as enabled by the approved supplier contract. A customer cancellation made in Klook is not received or processed by Mland V1 and does not release Mland capacity automatically; Klook's own notification is the operational signal to Staff or Manager. See [Klook evidence](07-evidences.md#e-07--klook-supplier-integration-and-availability).

Mland uses the paid Gemini API for AI support. Mland retains application-level text prompts and responses for at most 30 days, stores no AI image input after processing, and does not use AI to make custom-order decisions. Reference images for custom manufacturing are retained until pickup or GHTK handoff; rejected or withdrawn custom requests and independently classified images are deleted immediately after processing. Recipient and address data for GHTK delivery are deleted or irreversibly obscured 30 days after handoff. VNPay transaction references and business audit evidence are retained for five years; Mland stores no card data. These purposes and retention periods are stated in the general Terms and Privacy Policy rather than through feature-specific image-consent checkboxes. See [AI and retention evidence](07-evidences.md#e-05--gemini-data-terms-and-logging).

V1 excludes warehouse locations, serials, lots, stock movements, procurement, inventory ledgers, real-time stock guarantees, Guest retail checkout, address books, shipping quotes and tracking, delivery failures, returns, in-system cancellation, refunds, accounting, and payment-reconciliation workflows. Customers seeking cancellation or refund contact the shop directly.
