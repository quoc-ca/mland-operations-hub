## 1. Scope and Purpose

Mland V1 is an operations platform for a ring atelier. It connects customer discovery, workshop booking, personalised ring work, retail orders, and shop-side fulfilment without becoming a warehouse-management, ERP, accounting, or end-to-end logistics system. Report 3 remains the detailed source for use cases and rules; this section defines the V1 scope and operating boundaries. The rationale and traceability for the decisions below are in [07. Evidences](07-evidences.md).

V1 supports account and role-based access control, public catalogue, workshop packages and schedules, ring-design consultation, Member retail, loyalty points and vouchers, notifications, operational administration, and an executive revenue dashboard. Staff operates catalogue products, images, prices, publication, and manually maintained available-to-sell quantities. Managers operate workshop schedules, capacity, material availability, holiday exceptions, promotions, vouchers, dashboards, and business approvals. Admins manage accounts, assign one of the fixed roles, operating parameters, and approved integration credentials. Staff and Manager accounts require an invitation before activation; Members register themselves. Firebase sends Member verification and password-recovery emails. Mland SMTP sends internal invitations and business notifications.

Guests and Members can browse published products, workshop packages, schedules, available slots, ring models, and public review information. They can book a workshop after selecting a package and supported design path, and may use Basic AI Support for approved public catalogue, workshop, store-information, and FAQ questions. Basic AI Support is separate from Feature-004 and cannot assess images, set prices, confirm transactions, or make business decisions. A workshop booking group is confirmed only after a verified VNPay payment of its booking-time collection (each ticket up to 200,000 VND), or without VNPay when nothing has to be collected (Observed in the implementation and Report 3, 2026-11-25). Members may additionally maintain profiles, use retail checkout, use loyalty points or vouchers, submit human-reviewed custom design requests, and chat with Staff or Manager.

Guest and Member workshop bookings made through Mland may be cancelled when at least 24 hours remain before the scheduled session start. For an eligible cancellation of a confirmed booking with a paid deposit, Mland initiates a VNPay refund for 100% of that booking's deposit. A workshop session may be booked no more than 30 calendar days in advance. These rules apply to Mland workshop bookings. VNPay refund processing time and pending/failed refund handling are not specified in this baseline.

A workshop booking group is held for 12 minutes and its VNPay link expires 2 minutes before the hold ends. A retail checkout paid by VNPay is held for 10 minutes; an order paid in cash at the counter (store pickup only) is held for 3 days. A custom order has no capacity hold: its payment link lasts until the quote deadline. A signed VNPay IPN is the normal confirmation channel; Mland verifies the signature, transaction reference, amount, and successful transaction status before applying the state transition, and safely ignores duplicate events. If no valid IPN has been recorded while the resource is still held, Mland may use signed VNPay QueryDR as a recovery confirmation. A browser return URL never confirms payment. If no successful evidence exists when the hold ends, Mland releases the hold (a retail hold for which VNPay was opened is released only after VNPay reports the payment failed or expired); any later evidence is a payment exception for manual shop follow-up and never automatically triggers fulfilment. This is an operational recovery control, not an accounting or reconciliation workflow. See [payment evidence](07-evidences.md#e-02--vnpay-payment-and-ipn) and [QueryDR evidence](07-evidences.md#e-03--vnpay-querydr).

Member retail supports cart management, whole-cart revalidation, price snapshots, and a whole-cart hold (10 minutes for VNPay, 3 days for cash at the counter) (Observed in the implementation and Report 3, 2026-11-25). Invalid or unavailable cart lines are removed and reported; checkout creates no partial order. A verified VNPay full payment, or a counter cash amount recorded by Staff, completes the sale and deducts the held quantities. After payment, the Member selects store pickup or fulfilment by Giao Hàng Tiết Kiệm (GHTK). GHTK fulfilment requires recipient and delivery-address data for that order. Staff prepares the order and marks it ready; Staff records a store pickup, and the GHTK partner account records the courier pickup and delivery. V1 calls GHTK only for the delivery-fee quote and has no shipment, tracking, or webhook integration. See [E-01 and E-10](07-evidences.md#e-01--mland-business-decision-record).

V1 supports a full custom-manufacturing flow for Members. A Member submits a reference image or a system-supported manual design/configuration, chooses a fulfilment method, and may provide a requested deadline. Managers and Admins configure the minimum deadline and the dates on which deadlines cannot be selected. If the Member supplies no deadline, the system assigns the earliest valid date. After human review, the Member receives a Manager-approved quote. The Member pays the complete quote directly from the custom-order detail page; no Add to Cart action or deposit exists. No queue or capacity hold is created; the payment link lasts until the quote deadline.

Custom manufacturing uses one complete quoted amount. There is no later final-balance request, partial payment, or retail cart line. Verified full payment is required before production/fulfilment proceeds; an unpaid quote expires or cancels according to the configured deadline.

Google Maps Embed and Google Places are not used; they were removed from V1 (confirmed 2026-11-25). Mland shows no Google map, rating or review content and opens no Google review link.

Mland is the sole source of truth for workshop sessions and capacity. See [E-07](07-evidences.md).

Mland uses the paid Gemini API for AI support. Mland retains application-level text prompts and responses for at most 30 days, stores no AI image input after processing, and does not use AI to make custom-order decisions. Reference images for custom manufacturing are retained until pickup or GHTK handoff; rejected or withdrawn custom requests and independently classified images are deleted immediately after processing. Recipient and address data for GHTK delivery are deleted or irreversibly obscured 30 days after handoff. VNPay transaction references and business audit evidence are retained for five years; Mland stores no card data. These purposes and retention periods are stated in the general Terms and Privacy Policy rather than through feature-specific image-consent checkboxes. See [AI and retention evidence](07-evidences.md#e-05--gemini-data-terms-and-logging).

V1 excludes warehouse locations, serials, lots, stock movements, procurement, inventory ledgers, real-time stock guarantees, Guest retail checkout, shipping tracking, delivery failures, returns, retail/custom-order cancellation and refunds, accounting, and payment-reconciliation workflows. Members may maintain a delivery address book. The workshop cancellation and deposit-refund flow above is the limited exception; customers seeking other cancellations or refunds contact the shop directly.
