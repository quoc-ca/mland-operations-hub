## 7. Evidences and Decision Traceability

This section records why Report 2 adopts its integration and operating-boundary decisions. It distinguishes two evidence types:

- **Mland business decision record**: decisions confirmed by the Mland stakeholder during the clarification session on 05/10/2026. This is the authoritative source for business scope, provider selection, retention choices, timings, and exclusions.
- **Provider documentation**: official documentation used to verify a provider capability or restriction. It does not replace a Mland business decision.

### Reading this register

- A provider source proves only what the provider publicly supports or requires. It must not be used to infer a Mland price rule, deposit percentage, hold duration, retention period, or operational role.
- The 10-minute VNPay-link/15-minute hold split is a Mland control that uses VNPay's expiry capability to leave time for payment confirmation; it is not a VNPay-mandated duration.
- Klook cancellation is intentionally outside Mland V1. This avoids automatically freeing Mland capacity from an event the system does not receive, but can leave capacity unused until the shop handles Klook's notification.
- Before production launch, Admin must retain current contract, credential, billing, quota, and sandbox/UAT evidence for each enabled provider. Secrets and personal data must never be added to this register.
