## 4. Capacity Planning

This capacity model is illustrative and must be reviewed by SEP490_G22 before it is used as a delivery baseline. It assumes five team members, 15 working hours per person per week, and a 20% reserve for rework, coordination, and uncertainty.

| Item | Illustrative Value | Calculation / Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Team size | 5 people | Planning assumption supplied for this draft |
| Hours per person per week | 15 hours | Planning assumption |
| Team capacity per week | 75 hours | 5 people × 15 hours |
| Planning duration | 14 weeks | Use relative project weeks until dates are approved |
| Gross capacity | 1,050 hours / 131.25 person-days | 75 hours × 14 weeks; 8 hours per person-day |
| Reserve | 20% / 210 hours / 26.25 person-days | Held for rework and uncertainty |
| Effective working capacity | 840 hours / 105 person-days | Gross capacity less the reserve |

Actual availability may differ because of academic workload, leave, or changes in team membership. The Project Lead should reforecast the remaining work at each iteration review; actual hours are not yet recorded in this plan.
