## 4. Capacity Planning

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Hours per person per week | 15 h (3 h/day × 5 days) |
| Team total per week | 5 people × 15 h = 75 h/week |
| Total duration | 14 weeks |
| Gross capacity | 75 h × 14 = 1,050 h (131.25 pds) |
| Buffer (meetings, reviews, unplanned — 20%) | 1,050 h × 20% = 210 h (26.25 pds) |
| Effective working capacity | 1,050 h × 80% = 840 h (105 pds) |

Members study other courses in parallel, so 15 hours per week is used instead of a full-time 40 hours. The Team Leader reviews the remaining effort at the end of each iteration.
