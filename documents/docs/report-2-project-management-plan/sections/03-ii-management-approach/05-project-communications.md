## 5. Project Communications

The cadence below is a proposed lightweight communication plan for the 14-week example. The team should select a meeting channel that is available to all members and record decisions in the project workspace.

| Communication Item | Who / Target | Purpose | Proposed Frequency | Type, Tool, or Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Team coordination | All team members | Review progress, blockers, and near-term priorities | Weekly; 30 minutes | Short team meeting; record actions in GitHub Issues |
| Iteration planning and review | All team members and relevant stakeholders | Agree on cycle scope, demonstrate completed work, and collect feedback | Every two weeks | Planning/review session; record decisions and follow-up items |
| Requirements or business decision | Project Lead, Business Analyst, affected roles, and Mland stakeholder when needed | Resolve an assumption or approval that blocks delivery | As needed; raise at the next review if not urgent | Decision note linked from the relevant requirement or issue |
| Defect or delivery blocker | Affected contributor, QA, and Project Lead | Make impact and next action visible quickly | As soon as identified | GitHub Issue with reproduction details, severity, and owner role |
| Project status summary | SEP490_G22 and project reviewer | Summarize completed work, risks, and decisions required | At the end of each iteration | Concise written update linked to reviewed project artifacts |
