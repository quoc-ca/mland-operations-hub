## 3. Project Training Plan

The following short sessions are proposed for the illustrative 14-week plan. The team should adjust them to its selected implementation tools and members' existing skills; no unconfirmed product technology is prescribed here.

| Training Area | Participants | Proposed Timing and Duration | Expected Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project scope, roles, and requirements traceability | All team members | Weeks 1–2; 2 hours | Apply the approved scope and link work to requirements and acceptance criteria |
| Git, GitHub issues, and review workflow | All team members | Week 1; 2 hours | Submit reviewable changes and keep decisions and defects traceable |
| Privacy, retention, and integration credentials | Development and integration roles | Before integration work; 2 hours | Handle personal data and secrets according to the approved constraints |
| Test design, automation, and defect reporting | Development and QA roles | Before the first test cycle; 3 hours | Record repeatable tests, results, and defect evidence |
| Release and document handover | All team members | Week 13; 2 hours | Prepare reviewed deliverables and confirm unresolved items are visible |

These dates and durations are illustrative and should be confirmed during the first iteration.
