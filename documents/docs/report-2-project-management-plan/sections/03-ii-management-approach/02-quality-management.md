## 2. Quality Management

We will make quality part of each iteration rather than leave it until the end. Before work begins, the team will agree on observable acceptance criteria. Before review, the contributor will check the change against those criteria and ask another team member to review it. Code changes should include relevant automated tests, and requirement changes should update their linked use cases and acceptance tests.

The team will verify the main business controls in the approved requirements, including role permissions, capacity holds, payment confirmation, duplicate-event handling, privacy and retention rules, and manual fulfilment handoff. Defects will be recorded with steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, severity, and resolution evidence. Critical defects must be resolved and retested before handover; lower-severity defects must have an explicit disposition. The proposed release targets are illustrative until the group approves them.

The existing GitHub workflow can validate and build the documentation baseline. It does not prove that the product has been implemented or tested; product test results must be recorded only after the corresponding tests have run.
