# **4\. Project Communications**

The project records meetings, decisions, and follow-up work from the active baseline date of 15 September 2026 through the Git-first document workspace. Concise evidence is classified in project and requirements trackers rather than copied into meeting transcripts.

| Activity | Status | Purpose | Required Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| New baseline communications from 15 September 2026 | Active | Record scope decisions, evidence, research, and detailed-design discussions for the ring customer journey. | Time log only when a meeting occurs; Q&A, Open Issues, Risks, or RTW records as applicable. |

Every discovery note must be labelled **Observed**, **Stakeholder statement**, **Group decision**, or **Assumption**. Only approved group decisions change the baseline. Questions and unresolved requirements belong in Q&A and Open Issues; risks belong in the project risk tracker; formal baseline changes belong in Change Logs.

Any approved image or other evidence must be minimised and exclude customer and transaction identifiers that are not needed for analysis.
