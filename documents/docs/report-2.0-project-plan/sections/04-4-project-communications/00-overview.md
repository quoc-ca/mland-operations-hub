# **4\. Project Communications**

The project records discovery, decisions, and follow-up work through the Git-first document workspace. The Project Plan defines the communication approach; concise evidence is then classified in the project and requirements trackers rather than copied into meeting transcripts.

| Activity | Status | Purpose | Required Record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Internal discovery, 09 September 2026, 20:20–21:00 | Completed | Discuss basic workflows, document management, and questions for the lecturer. | Time log plus open questions; no approved scope decision. |
| Internal discovery, 10 September 2026, 20:20–21:00 | Completed | Discuss finance, tax, social-insurance, and employment-contract concerns. | Time log, scope-boundary issue, and delivery risk; no approved requirement. |
| Workshop discovery, 12 September 2026, from 09:30 | Scheduled | Observe and interview the owner, manager, and staff about booking, personnel, inventory, finance, reporting, and forecasting. | Questions and answers, open issues, and evidence classification. |
| Lecturer review, 12 September 2026, 21:30 | Scheduled | Review direction, assessment expectations, delivery milestones, and scope feasibility. | Questions and answers; change record only if the team changes an approved baseline. |

Every discovery note must be labelled **Observed**, **Stakeholder statement**, **Group decision**, or **Assumption**. Only approved group decisions change the baseline. Questions and unresolved requirements belong in Q&A and Open Issues; risks belong in the project risk tracker; formal baseline changes belong in Change Logs.

During workshop discovery, the team may record approved photographs or audio only after consent and must remove customer, transaction, and personnel identifiers that are not needed for analysis.
