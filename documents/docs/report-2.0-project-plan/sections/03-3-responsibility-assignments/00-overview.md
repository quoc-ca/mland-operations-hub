# **3\. Responsibility Assignments**

The Mland Project Team makes collective scope and business-rule decisions and records each resulting baseline change. The workshop owner, manager, and staff are consulted sources for business context, operating evidence, and exceptions; their statements are not silently converted into approved requirements.

The lecturer reviews direction, milestones, and academic deliverables. Lecturer feedback does not replace the project team's scope decision process.
