# Software Requirements Specification

**Mland Operations Hub (MOH) — Report 3**

> **Active project, under validation.** Requirements remain subject to stakeholder validation.

**Known Mland context:** MOH V1 covers Guest and Member ring-workshop booking, Member ready-ring retail, feasibility triage, invoicing, fulfilment, and auditable payment records.

> **Stakeholder statement — 16 September 2026:** proposed daily sessions are 09:30–12:00, 13:00–15:30, and 16:00–18:30. Staff propose to confirm actual product participants at check-in, create continuation bookings for held work in progress, and use a research-gated Google Reviews dashboard for owner/manager review. These inputs are not approved requirements.

> **Group decision — 17 September 2026, informed by lecturer direction:** Guest and Member both book and pay a 50% package deposit through the Payment Gateway; the balance plus consented staff adjustments is settled cash/bank at the shop. Only Member purchases ready rings, pays in full online, and chooses pickup or third-party delivery handoff. Admin Technical has technical-only access.

> **TBD — Mland decision required:** per-location capacity/capability, cancellation/no-show and refund policy, attendance-fee exceptions, custody rules, package/catalogue data, consent wording, reservation expiry, delivery-data retention and handoff process, feasibility constraints and thresholds, review coverage, retention, Google review/payroll governance, and non-functional targets.
