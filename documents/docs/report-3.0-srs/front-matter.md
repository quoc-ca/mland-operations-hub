# Software Requirements Specification

**Personalized Product Sales and Workshop Booking System — Report 3**

> **Active project, under validation.** Requirements remain subject to stakeholder validation.

| Project information | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Group | SEP490_G22 |
| English project name | Personalized Product Sales and Workshop Booking System |

**Known Mland context:** V1 covers Guest and Member ring-workshop booking, Member ready-ring retail, feasibility triage, invoicing, fulfilment, and auditable payment records.

> **Stakeholder statement — 16 September 2026:** proposed daily sessions are 09:30–12:00, 13:00–15:30, and 16:00–18:30. Staff propose to confirm actual product participants at check-in, create continuation bookings for held work in progress, and use a research-gated Google Reviews dashboard for owner/manager review. These inputs were partly approved by the group decision on 18 September 2026.

> **Group decision — 17 September 2026, informed by lecturer direction:** Guest and Member both book and pay a 50% package deposit through the Payment Gateway; the balance plus consented staff adjustments is settled cash/bank at the shop. Only Member purchases ready rings, pays in full online, and chooses pickup or third-party delivery handoff. Admin Technical has technical-only access.

> **Group decision — 18 September 2026:** session schedule, location/session capacity configuration, 24-hour rescheduling, audited no-show exceptions, participant-only extra-attendee fee, custody/continuation evidence, Owner-confirmed catalogue path, AI triage governance, image lifecycle, conditional Google review import, and no-expiry ready-ring reservation are approved as stated in the SRS business rules.

> **TBD — group decision required:** exact location capacity/capability values; component/package data, prices and hard constraints; numeric feasibility thresholds and evaluation evidence; consent wording, provider retention and review-operation coverage; manual-refund/force-majeure policy; Google Review OAuth/API access plus retention/attribution/appeal policy; delivery-recipient retention and failed-handoff handling; Payment Gateway provider, webhook/IPN, reconciliation, retry, duplicate-event and refund rules; and non-functional targets.
