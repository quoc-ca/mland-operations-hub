# **How to Use This Template**

Use this SRS as the approved requirement baseline. During discovery, label every input as **Observed**, **Stakeholder statement**, **Group decision**, or **Assumption** and retain its source in the Requirements Traceability Workbook.

Only an approved group decision may become a functional requirement, business rule, data requirement, or non-functional requirement. Do not infer finance, personnel, inventory, forecasting, technical, or legal requirements from an informal discussion.
