# **Part 0 — Document Overview**

This SRS initially covers the 15 September 2026 V1 baseline: Guest and Member group-session booking, a 50% package deposit through Payment Gateway, optional ring design, feasibility triage, estimate, two fulfilment modes, auditable invoice adjustment/final-price consent, and manual cash/bank final settlement. Members can view participation/transaction history and upcoming workshop events, and can buy ready-made rings after full online payment.

The 16 September stakeholder inputs propose guest location selection, three-session operating hours, staff-confirmed additional-participant fees, and staff-created continuation bookings with work-in-progress custody; these remain pending approval and detailed rules. Ready-ring retail reserves one unit after full payment and supports pickup or third-party delivery handoff only. No carrier integration, live shipment tracking, return, or shipping-refund process is included.

Google Reviews visibility is a research-gated owner/manager dashboard candidate, not an approved payroll or bonus automation feature. Admin Technical manages technical configuration, integration monitoring, and technical audit only. Staff/HR management, broader retail, full inventory, production scheduling, accounting, and CAD/3D tooling are excluded.

Each accepted requirement must map to a Vision & Scope feature, use case or scenario, business rule, data requirement, non-functional requirement where applicable, design artefact, and test evidence through the Requirements Traceability Workbook.
