# **Part 0 — Document Overview**

This SRS covers the 15 September 2026 V1 baseline and group decisions through 18 September 2026: Guest and Member group-session booking, a 50% package deposit through Payment Gateway, optional ring design, feasibility triage, estimate, two fulfilment modes, auditable invoice adjustment/final-price consent, and manual cash/bank final settlement. Members can view participation/transaction history and upcoming workshop events, and can buy ready-made rings after full online payment.

The approved operating day has three sessions (09:30–12:00, 13:00–15:30, and 16:00–18:30), with capacity configured per location/session. Rescheduling requires at least 24 hours and an available session; no-show exceptions require Staff or Owner audit. Staff attest extra product participants at check-in and may create continuation bookings with custody evidence only on customer request and available capacity. Ready-ring retail reserves one unit without automatic expiry after full payment and supports pickup or evidenced third-party delivery handoff only. No carrier integration, live shipment tracking, return, or shipping-refund process is included.

The website offers a Google review link/QR for customer-authored reviews. Conditional on verified Business Profile access and approved governance, Owner may manually import reviews one-way for Owner and public website visibility; this is not payroll or bonus automation. The Payment Gateway provider and all webhook/IPN, reconciliation, retry, duplicate-event, and refund rules remain open. Admin Technical manages technical configuration, integration monitoring, and technical audit only. Staff/HR management, broader retail, full inventory, production scheduling, accounting, and CAD/3D tooling are excluded.

Each accepted requirement must map to a Vision & Scope feature, use case or scenario, business rule, data requirement, non-functional requirement where applicable, design artefact, and test evidence through the Requirements Traceability Workbook.
