# I. Project Overview {#project-overview}

The Personalized Product Sales and Workshop Booking System is a web-based system developed for Mland, a jewellery workshop that combines personalised ring-making experiences with ready-made ring sales. A customer may begin with a workshop booking, an initial idea, a reference image, a component request, or a request for the shop to make a custom ring.

The central business pain point is **customization**, not simply the lack of an online booking calendar. A customised ring is not a fixed catalogue item: the customer may change its material, gemstone, shape, or other parameters while the idea is being discussed. Mland must convert that input into a clear design context and assess it against available components, workshop capability, expected effort, and price. Today, this information is spread across messages, calls, images, informal notes, and separate booking or quotation conversations. Staff therefore spend time clarifying requirements and reconstructing the request, while design input, feasibility reasoning, estimates, booking details, and invoice adjustments are not consistently kept as one record. This creates a gap between what the customer imagines, what the shop can make, and what it can accurately quote and deliver.

V1 addresses this gap through one bilingual solution connecting booking, design input, feasibility review, estimation, payment, fulfilment, and history. Customers may select an existing design, configure available components, submit a reference image with consent, or consult Staff in person. An external AI Vision API may extract candidate features, but it does not generate the final design or determine the price. The feasibility engine remains a research prototype under validation, with Staff and Owner responsible for required reviews and authorised overrides. The system thus adds structure and traceability without assuming that customization can be fully automated.

The V1 baseline focuses on ring customization, and Member purchases of ready-made rings. It excludes autonomous design generation, exact image reconstruction, CAD/3D editing, full inventory management, detailed production planning, HR management, accounting, and complete delivery tracking.
