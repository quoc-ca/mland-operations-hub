# I. Project Overview {#project-overview}

The Personalized Product Sales and Workshop Booking System is a web-based bilingual system for Mland, a jewellery workshop that combines personalised ring-making experiences with ready-made ring sales. Guests can discover workshops, products and public information, while active Members can configure and save a ring design, submit a custom design request, consult Staff or Manager, book a workshop, or purchase a ready-made ring. Protected design, consultation and custom-order actions require Member sign-in.

The central business pain point is **customization**, not simply the lack of an online booking calendar. A customized ring is not a fixed catalogue item: the customer may change its material, gemstone, shape, engraving, or other parameters while the idea is being discussed. Mland must preserve that input, check image quality where images are provided, assess feasibility, explain the decision, prepare a suitable quote when manufacturing is requested, and connect the result to payment and fulfilment. Today, these details are spread across messages, calls, images, informal notes, and separate booking or quotation conversations. Staff therefore spend time clarifying requirements and reconstructing the request, while design input, review reasoning, quote history, booking details, and fulfilment records are not consistently kept together.

V1 addresses this gap through one bilingual solution connecting workshop discovery, ring configuration, design-request review, quotation, payment, fulfilment, and customer-visible history. Guests can see the available journeys and public information, while only active Members can submit a design request with a description and/or up to five images. Image quality is checked before submission; Staff or Manager then accepts, rejects, or requests clarification and must record an explanation. Feature-004 does not use AI to analyse the design, suggest a price, or make a business decision.

The V1 baseline distinguishes three paths: a workshop ring made by the customer through the approved workshop-booking design path; a shop-made custom-manufacturing order requested by an eligible Member after review and quotation; and a ready-made ring purchased from the catalogue. Feature-012 is an independent configurator: after a Member saves a design, the system may recommend a workshop package, but the recommendation does not create a booking, hide other workshops, or change payment or order state. Feature-013 provides Member consultation with Staff or Manager when the customer needs guidance. The baseline excludes autonomous design generation, exact image reconstruction, CAD/3D editing, full inventory management, detailed production planning, HR management, accounting, and complete delivery tracking.
