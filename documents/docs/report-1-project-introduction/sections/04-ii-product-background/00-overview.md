# II. Product Background {#product-background}

Mland is a jewellery-making workshop focused on personalised ring experiences. A customer may attend a group workshop to make a workshop ring. An eligible Member may separately request that the shop make a custom-manufactured ring after human review and quotation. Customers may also buy ready-made rings from the published catalogue.

Today, booking requests and initial quotes are handled manually through channels such as Instagram and WhatsApp. Customers may send an incomplete description, preferred materials or components, and reference images, then change their requirements during the discussion. Staff must check image quality, clarify the request, distinguish a workshop ring from a shop-made custom-manufacturing order, assess feasibility, prepare a quote, and follow up on payment and fulfilment. Because these details are spread across messages, calls, images, and notes, customers lack one bilingual path for design input, review, payment, and permitted status viewing. Staff must repeatedly reconstruct the design, review, booking, and quotation context.

V1 provides a bilingual solution from workshop discovery through ring customization, human review, quotation, payment, and fulfilment. Feature-012 lets customers configure and save a workshop-ring design and receive an advisory package recommendation. Feature-004 lets Guest and Member submit a design request for Staff or Manager review; it does not use AI. An accepted Member request may continue through the separate custom-order quote and full-payment workflow.
