# II Product Background {#ii-product-background}

Mland is a jewellery-making workshop focused on personalised ring experiences. A customer may attend a group workshop to make a ring or request that the shop make a custom ring after consultation.

Today, booking requests and initial quotes are handled manually through channels such as Instagram and WhatsApp. Customers have no single bilingual path to reserve a workshop session, explore a feasible ring design, receive an estimate, or follow an order. Staff must reconstruct the relevant design and booking context before advising the customer.

V1 establishes one customer-facing path from booking to ring design and fulfilment. It does not attempt to replace staff management, inventory, detailed production planning, or accounting.
