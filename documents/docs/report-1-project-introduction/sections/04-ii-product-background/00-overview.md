# II. Product Background {#product-background}

Mland is a jewellery-making workshop focused on personalised ring experiences. A customer may attend a group workshop to make a workshop ring. An eligible Member may separately request that the shop make a custom-manufactured ring after consultation.

Today, booking requests and initial quotes are handled manually through channels such as Instagram and WhatsApp. Customers may send an idea or preferred materials and components, then change their requirements during the discussion; an eligible Member may also submit a reference image for review. Staff must clarify the request, check its feasibility, distinguish a workshop ring from a shop-made custom-manufacturing order, and prepare an estimate. Because these details are spread across messages, calls, images, and notes, customers lack one bilingual path for booking, design exploration, payment, and permitted order-status viewing. Staff must repeatedly reconstruct the design, feasibility, booking, and quotation context, making changes difficult to track consistently.

V1 provides a bilingual solution from workshop booking through ring customization, feasibility review, estimate, payment, and fulfilment. Customers may configure a workshop ring from supported components; only Members may submit a reference image for AI-assisted candidate-feature analysis or create a shop-made custom-manufacturing order. AI output and price suggestions are advisory, while Staff and Manager retain business judgement.
