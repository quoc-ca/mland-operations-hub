# II. Product Background {#product-background}

Mland is a jewellery-making workshop focused on personalised ring experiences. A customer may attend a group workshop to make a ring or request that the shop make a custom ring after consultation.

Today, booking requests and initial quotes are handled manually through channels such as Instagram and WhatsApp. Customers may send an idea, reference image, or preferred materials and components, then change their requirements during the discussion. Staff must clarify the request, check its feasibility, decide whether the ring will be made in a workshop or by the shop, and prepare an estimate. Because these details are spread across messages, calls, images, and notes, customers lack one bilingual path for booking, design exploration, payment, and order tracking. Staff must repeatedly reconstruct the design, feasibility, booking, and quotation context, making changes difficult to track consistently.

V1 provides a bilingual solution from workshop booking through ring customization, feasibility review, estimate, payment, and fulfilment. Customers may configure a design or submit a reference image; AI supports feature extraction and routing, while Staff and Owner retain judgement.
