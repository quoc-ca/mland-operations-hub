# III. Existing Solutions {#existing-solutions}

The ring-customization workflow proposed for Mland and Domino's “Build Your Own Pizza” feature share the same basic product-design principle: a customer starts with a base product and personalises it by selecting from a controlled set of components. Domino's guides the customer through choices such as size, crust, sauce, and toppings, while Mland's workflow guides the customer through ring models, materials, gemstones, shapes, engraving, and available components. In both cases, customers make decisions step by step instead of describing the entire product informally to an employee.

The important difference is uncertainty and accountability. A pizza configuration is usually validated immediately against a predefined menu and production process. A customised ring may begin with an incomplete description or reference images, require image-quality validation and clarification, and need Staff or Manager review before quotation or manufacture. Domino's demonstrates guided customization and immediate option-based ordering; Mland extends that idea into a traceable workflow connecting customization with workshop discovery, human review, quotation, payment, fulfilment, and customer-visible explanations. No AI decision is assumed in the current workflow.
