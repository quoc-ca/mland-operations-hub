## 1. Major Features {#major-features}

| ID | Feature Name | Description | Addresses Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FE-01 | Account Management | Handles user registration, secure authentication via Single Sign-On (SSO) Identity Providers (Google/Facebook) or standard credentials, profile management, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across different user roles. | GAP-01 |
| FE-02 | Product Management | Handles published retail jewellery catalogues, supported customizable component listings, manually maintained available-to-sell quantities, and purchasing of ready-made ring models exclusively for authenticated Members with 100% upfront payment through the Payment Gateway. It does not provide full inventory, stock movement, procurement, or warehouse management. | GAP-01 |
| FE-03 | Workshop Management | Manages the end-to-end workshop lifecycle, with two branches and three daily session templates maintained as configurable operational data. It covers workshop program and package management, group booking, mandatory 50% online deposits, participant capacity, attendance tracking, and check-in operations. | GAP-01 |
| FE-04 | AI Chatbox Analysis & Suggestion | Provides basic text support and, for an eligible Member's consented reference image, candidate ring-feature and image-quality analysis. It may provide advisory price suggestions for new workshop packages or retail products, but it does not generate the final design, determine final prices, accept or reject custom requests, or review custom-manufacturing orders. | GAP-01 |
| FE-05 | Personalized Design & Quote Workflow | Manages two separate custom-ring paths: a workshop ring configured from supported components and made by the customer during a booked session, and a shop-made custom-manufacturing order submitted by an eligible Member. It records design inputs, material or gemstone adjustments, feasibility information, estimate context, customer clarification, and routing to Staff or Manager review when business judgement is required. AI-generated price suggestions are advisory only; the final estimate and financial decision are made by the authorised business role. | GAP-01 |
| FE-06 | Loyalty & Promotion Management | Tracks customer transaction histories to automatically compute reward points, manages membership tier progression, and provisions promotional benefit campaigns and discount vouchers for Members. | GAP-01 |
| FE-07 | Notification Management | Centralizes multi-channel dispatches via the Mail Gateway, including deposit confirmation emails, security verification codes, digital QR code check-in tickets, order status updates, and internal Manager approval request notifications. | GAP-01 |
| FE-08 | Order Management & Fulfillment | Manages ready-made ring and eligible custom-manufacturing fulfilment through Member pickup or Staff-recorded manual handoff to GHTK. It records preparation, pickup, handoff evidence, permitted order status, and approved financial adjustments. V1 does not integrate with carrier APIs, calculate delivery fees, track shipments, or manage delivery failure, return, or shipping-refund workflows. | GAP-01 |
| FE-09 | Administrative Parameter Configuration | Separates business and technical authority. The Manager configures or approves business and financial parameters within the approved workflow, such as prices, capacity, materials, queue rules, and approval thresholds. Admin Technical manages accounts, RBAC, technical operational parameters, integration credentials, monitoring, and technical audit, but does not make routine business or financial decisions. | GAP-01 |
