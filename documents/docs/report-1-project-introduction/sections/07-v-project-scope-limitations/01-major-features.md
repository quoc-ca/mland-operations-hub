## 1 Major Features

| ID | Feature Name | Description | Addresses Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FE-01 | Guest and Member Workshop Booking | Guest and Member choose a location and one of three daily sessions. Capacity is configured per location/session; rescheduling requires 24 hours and capacity, while no-show exceptions are audited. Every package creates an invoice with a 50% online deposit; a confirmed gateway transaction confirms the booking. | GAP-01 |
| FE-02 | Ring Catalogue and Configuration | Customers choose a confirmed ring model or Owner-confirmed components with current price, difficulty, and hard constraints; Staff can continue design consultation in person. | GAP-01 |
| FE-03 | Image-Assisted Feature Intake | With explicit consent, a reference image is sent to an external AI Vision API for quality and candidate-feature extraction. Accepted requests are retained until order completion; rejected or withdrawn requests are deleted. | GAP-01 |
| FE-04 | Feasibility Triage and Override Audit | The engine routes custom designs as simple/auto-accept, medium/Staff review, advanced/Owner review, or impossible/auto-reject from configured feasibility rules; overrides record their reason and decision context. | GAP-01 |
| FE-05 | Invoice, Deposit and Final-Price Consent | The system records the package invoice, 50% deposit transaction, auditable Staff adjustments, and customer consent before the final balance is settled at the shop. | GAP-01 |
| FE-06 | Ring Fulfilment, Continuation and Collection | Customers choose self-making at the workshop or a shop-made order; Staff can record auditable custody and create a linked continuation booking on customer request and available capacity. | GAP-01 |
| FE-07 | Check-in and Final Settlement Record | Staff attest actual participants at check-in and record cash/bank settlement. The 100,000 VND fee applies only to each unbooked extra product participant; its adjustment needs customer consent, and Owner approves a waiver or change. | GAP-01 |
| FE-08 | Google Reviews Visibility | The website offers a Google review link or QR; after verified access and governance approval, Owner may manually import reviews one-way from Google for Owner and public website visibility. It must not automate pay or bonus decisions. | GAP-01 |
| FE-09 | Member Ready-Ring Retail | Only Members can pay in full online for a ready-made ring, reserve one unit without automatic expiry, and select pickup or evidenced third-party delivery handoff. | GAP-01 |
| FE-10 | Technical Administration | Admin Technical manages technical configuration, integration monitoring, and technical audit without business-record mutation. | GAP-01 |
