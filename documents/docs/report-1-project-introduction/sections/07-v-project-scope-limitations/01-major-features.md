## 1. Major Features {#major-features}

> **Feature-004 baseline update (2026-11-20):** The former AI Chatbox Analysis & Suggestion entry is superseded by **Design Request and Human Review**. Guest and Member may submit up to five images per independent request; Staff and Manager perform the accountable review and must provide an explanation. No AI analysis, AI pricing, or automated decision belongs to Feature-004.

> **Cross-feature update (2026-11-22):** Feature-012 no longer attaches a
> configured design to a workshop ticket. After saving, it recommends a
> workshop package without blocking other choices. Feature-004 may use an
> owner-visible design snapshot, and Feature-013 can provide consultation for
> Guest or Member with Staff/Manager participation.

| FE-01 | Account Management                     | Handles user registration, secure authentication via Single Sign-On (SSO) Identity Providers (Google/Facebook) or standard credentials, profile management, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across different user roles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | GAP-06         |
| FE-02 | Product Management                     | Manages ready-made ring products approved for sale, including product information, images, variants, prices, publication status, and manually maintained available-to-sell quantities. Customers may browse, search, and filter published products by predefined attributes. Authenticated Members may purchase eligible ready-made rings at the displayed price with 100% upfront payment through the Payment Gateway. Custom designs requiring feasibility review, quotation, approval, one-time full payment, or shop manufacturing are handled through the Personalized Design & Quote Workflow. It does not provide full inventory, stock movement, procurement, warehouse, or production management. | GAP-01, GAP-05 |
| FE-03 | Workshop Management                    | Manages the end-to-end workshop lifecycle, with two branches and three daily session templates maintained as configurable operational data. | FE-04 | Design Request & Human Review           | Allows Guest and Member to submit an independent design request with a description and/or up to five images. Backend quality validation blocks blurry images; Staff and Manager independently accept or reject with a mandatory explanation, and the request owner can track the recorded outcome. No AI analysis, AI pricing, booking, payment, or automated decision is included.                                                                                                                               | GAP-03         |
| FE-05 | Personalized Design & Quote Workflow   | Manages two separate custom-ring paths: a workshop ring configured from supported components and made by the customer during a booked session, and a shop-made custom-manufacturing order submitted by an eligible Member. It records design inputs, material or gemstone adjustments, feasibility information, estimate context, customer clarification, and routing to Staff or Manager review when business judgement is required. AI-generated price suggestions are advisory only; the final estimate and financial decision are made by the authorised business role. | GAP-01, GAP-03 |
| FE-06 | Loyalty & Promotion Management         | Tracks customer transaction histories to automatically compute reward points, manages membership tier progression, and provisions promotional benefit campaigns and discount vouchers for Members.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | GAP-07         |
| FE-07 | Notification Management                | Centralizes multi-channel dispatches via the Mail Gateway, including deposit confirmation emails, security verification codes, digital QR code check-in tickets, order status updates, and internal Manager approval request notifications.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | GAP-04, GAP-05 |
| FE-08 | Order Management & Fulfillment         | Manages ready-made ring and eligible On-Demand Order fulfilment through Member pickup or Staff-recorded manual handoff to GHTK. It records preparation, pickup, handoff evidence, permitted order status, and approved financial adjustments. V1 does not integrate with carrier APIs, calculate delivery fees, track shipments, or manage delivery failure, return, or shipping-refund workflows.                                                                                                                                                                          | GAP-04         |
| FE-09 | Administrative Parameter Configuration | Separates business and technical authority. The Manager configures or approves business and financial parameters within the approved workflow, such as prices, capacity, materials, queue rules, and approval thresholds. Admin Technical manages accounts, RBAC, technical operational parameters, integration credentials, monitoring, and technical audit, but does not make routine business or financial decisions.                                                                                                                                                    | GAP-06         |
| FE-10 | Manager Dashboard                      | Provides role-based dashboards for Managers to monitor workshop bookings, participant attendance, retail and custom-manufacturing orders, payment and revenue summaries, fulfilment progress, approval queues, and operational exceptions. The dashboard supports filtering by date, branch, order type, and order status. It does not provide inventory, warehouse, procurement, accounting, carrier tracking, or delivery-management functionality.                                                                                                                       | GAP-04         |
