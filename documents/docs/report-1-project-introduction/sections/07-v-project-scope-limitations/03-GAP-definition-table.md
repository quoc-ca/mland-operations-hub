## Gap Definition Table {#gap-definition-table}

| Gap Code | Gap Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GAP-01 | Fragmented Customization Context | Customer ideas, component choices, reference information, feasibility decisions, estimates, and later changes are spread across messages, images, calls, and informal notes instead of being maintained as one traceable customization record. |
| GAP-02 | Disconnected Workshop Booking Coordination | Customers do not have one bilingual path to select a branch, session, package, participant information, capacity, and deposit while Staff maintain a consistent view of availability and check-in requirements. |
| GAP-03 | Unstructured Feasibility and Estimation | Mland lacks a structured way to assess whether a requested ring can be made, route the request to the appropriate Staff or Manager review, and preserve the context behind an estimate. AI assistance can support analysis or suggestions but cannot replace the authorised business decision. |
| GAP-04 | Disconnected Payment and Fulfilment Traceability | Payment results, invoice adjustments, order status, custody, pickup, and manual delivery handoff are difficult to connect across the customer request and operational record, making settlement and fulfilment follow-up inconsistent. |
| GAP-05 | Limited Customer Self-Service and Status Visibility | Customers lack a single bilingual path to review available products or workshops, complete permitted actions, receive confirmations, and look up booking or order status without repeatedly contacting Staff. |
| GAP-06 | Inconsistent Role and Decision Governance | Business, financial, operational, and technical responsibilities are not consistently separated, making it difficult to identify who may review a design, approve an adjustment, manage operational rules, or change technical configuration. |
| GAP-07 | Limited Member Promotion and Retention Support | Member transactions, reward benefits, promotion campaigns, vouchers, and membership progression are not managed through one consistent system process. |
