## Gap Definition Table {#gap-definition-table}

::: {.docx-table width="100%" columns="20%,20%,60%" repeat-header="false"}

| Gap Code | Gap Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GAP-01 | Fragmented Customization Context | Customer ideas, component choices, reference images, saved configurations, review outcomes, quotes, and later changes are spread across messages and informal notes instead of being maintained as one traceable customization record. |
| GAP-02 | Disconnected Workshop Booking Coordination | Customers do not have one bilingual path to discover a package, configure a ring, select a branch and session, submit participant information, and complete the approved deposit while Staff maintain a consistent view of capacity and check-in requirements. |
| GAP-03 | Unstructured Design Review and Quotation | Mland lacks a structured way to validate image quality, route a design request to Staff or Manager, preserve the mandatory explanation, prepare a quote, and expose the resulting status to the customer. |
| GAP-04 | Disconnected Payment and Fulfilment Traceability | Payment results, quote expiry, invoice adjustments, order status, custody, pickup, and manual delivery handoff are difficult to connect across the customer request and operational record. |
| GAP-05 | Limited Customer Self-Service and Status Visibility | Customers lack a single bilingual path to configure designs, submit requests, receive review explanations, pay approved quotes, and look up booking or order status without repeatedly contacting Staff. |
| GAP-06 | Inconsistent Role and Decision Governance | Business, financial, operational, and technical responsibilities are not consistently separated, making it difficult to identify who may review a design, approve a quote, manage operational rules, or change technical configuration. |
| GAP-07 | Limited Member Promotion and Retention Support | Member transactions, reward benefits, promotion campaigns, vouchers, and membership progression are not managed through one consistent system process. |

:::
