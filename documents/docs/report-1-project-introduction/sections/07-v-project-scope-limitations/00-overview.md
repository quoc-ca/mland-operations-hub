# V. Project Scope & Limitations {#project-scope-limitations}

The active V1 baseline begins on 15 September 2026. It is centred on personalised rings and separates workshop participation, shop-made custom manufacturing, and ready-made retail. The scope includes ring configuration, human design-request review, quotation, payment, fulfilment, and customer-visible history. Two branches and three daily session templates are configurable operational data, not hard-coded availability; exact capacities, branch capabilities, and unresolved payment or governance rules remain open rather than becoming commitments.

Feature-012 recommendations do not create bookings or alter payment and order state. Feature-004 does not use AI. Custom orders require one approved quote and full payment by the quote deadline, while workshop bookings retain their separate deposit and settlement rules. Any change requires a recorded group decision and a corresponding update to requirements traceability.
