## 2. Limitations & Exclusions {#limitations-exclusions}

::: {.docx-table width="100%" columns="10%,25%,65%" repeat-header="false"}

| ID | Excluded Item | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LI-01 | Staff and HR management | Shift scheduling, payroll, and contracts are not part of Mland's identified gaps. V1 only assigns Staff to branches and sessions. |
| LI-02 | Inventory and production planning | V1 tracks sellable quantities only. Warehouses, procurement, stock movements, and detailed production scheduling would require an ERP-level system. |
| LI-03 | Accounting and automatic refunds | V1 records payments and refunds but is not an accounting system. Refunds are made by bank transfer outside the system; tax, POS, and gateway refunds are excluded. |
| LI-04 | Cancellation of retail and custom orders | Only workshop tickets can be cancelled or changed in the system. Other cancellations are handled directly by the shop. |
| LI-05 | AI design analysis and AI chatbot | Design decisions and prices must stay with Staff and Manager. V1 contains no AI image analysis, AI pricing, or AI customer chat. |
| LI-06 | CAD/3D design tools | A step-by-step configurator meets Mland's needs; CAD/3D editing and exact reconstruction from photos are excluded. |
| LI-07 | Shipment tracking and returns | GHTK is used only for delivery-fee quotes. Shipment creation, live tracking, failed delivery, and returns are deferred. |
| LI-08 | SMS, Zalo, and push notifications | Email covers V1 needs. Other channels require additional provider contracts. |
| LI-09 | Guest private actions | Guests can browse and book workshops, but saving designs, design requests, chat, retail purchase, and reviews require a Member account. |
| LI-10 | Jewellery other than rings, native mobile app | V1 focuses on rings and a responsive web application. Other products and native apps are deferred to later versions. |

:::
