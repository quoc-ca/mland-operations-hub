# IV Proposed Solution {#iv-proposed-solution}

The Personalized Product Sales and Workshop Booking System is a bilingual system with Guest and Member journeys. Both may select a workshop location and reserve a group workshop session, then choose an existing ring design, configure available ring components, submit a reference image with consent, or wait to consult Staff in person. The approved operating day has sessions at 09:30-12:00, 13:00-15:30, and 16:00-18:30. Capacity is configured for each location and session; its exact values and location product capabilities remain operational data to approve.

For a reference image or custom design outside the confirmed catalogue, an external AI Vision API extracts candidate ring features only. The feasibility engine uses Owner-configured component scores, hard constraints, and thresholds to route a request as simple/auto-accept, medium/Staff review, advanced/Owner review, or impossible/auto-reject. The engine is a research prototype under validation: it does not generate designs or decide prices. Authorised Staff or Owner overrides require a recorded reason and decision context; numeric values and evaluation evidence remain open.

Every workshop booking creates an invoice for its package and directs the Guest or Member to pay a 50% deposit through the Payment Gateway. Only a verified gateway payment is recorded as a transaction and confirms the booking. The gateway provider, webhook/IPN contract, reconciliation, retry, duplicate-event handling, and refunds remain unresolved. After a feasible design is available, the system shows a price estimate from the staff-maintained component catalogue. The customer can either make the ring at the workshop or place a shop-made custom order. Shop-made orders use simple fulfilment statuses through pickup. At check-in, Staff confirm people actually participating in making the product; the 100,000 VND attendance fee applies only to each unbooked additional participant. The invoice adjustment requires customer consent; Owner approval and a Staff-recorded reason are required for a waiver or fee change.

Staff may add an adjustment only with its reason, actor, time, and customer consent retained in the invoice audit trail. The final workshop balance is the unpaid 50% of the package plus approved adjustments; Staff record its cash or bank-transfer settlement at the shop. A gateway redirect alone is not treated as payment confirmation; the system requires a gateway status or transaction reconciliation.

Some workshop products may require more than one session. Staff create a continuation booking only on customer request and when a later session has capacity. Staff record a work-item description, intake photo, location and time, and actor; receipt and release are auditable, and release requires the booking code or QR plus Staff verification. The system does not automatically reserve a next session or allow the Guest to create a continuation booking without Staff action.

Members may also buy a ready-made ring. The system collects full online payment through the Payment Gateway before reserving one unit; the reservation has no automatic expiry in V1 and ends by pickup or carrier handoff. The Member chooses pickup or a Staff-recorded handoff to a third-party delivery provider. Handoff evidence includes carrier, handoff reference, Staff actor, and timestamp. The system stops at handoff: carrier integration, live shipment status, delivery failure, returns, and shipping-refund logistics are out of scope; recipient-data retention and failed-handoff handling remain open.

The system sends booking and review or order notifications by email and lets Guests use a tracking code; Members can view participation history, transaction history, and upcoming workshop events. Loyalty is deferred. The website presents a Google review link or QR for customer-authored reviews. Owner may manually trigger a one-way Google Business Profile import only after verified access and approval of retention, attribution, approval, and appeal policy; imported reviews may be public on the website and visible to Owner. This feature must not automatically calculate employee pay or bonuses. Admin Technical manages technical configuration, integration monitoring, and technical audit but cannot mutate business records. The system does not store card data, schedule Staff, manage full inventory, or perform detailed production planning.
